# Hindi and Russian: Joshua to 2 Chronicles

**Status: proposed, awaiting native Hindi and Russian reviewers.** These are
the choices used to draft Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1-2 Samuel, 1-2 Kings and 1-2
Chronicles in Hindi and Russian, so that chapter blocks drafted in parallel
read as one translation. `docs/pentateuch-hindi-russian-terms.md` still
holds in full (the divine name and its setting, forms of address, measures,
cultic terms, names): this note adds what the history books need. Where the
Pentateuch and New Testament in this translation already have a form, that
form wins. If a choice here changes, every passage drafted under it is
revised. Decision text stays in English, quoting the Hindi or Russian words.

## Order and parallels

- **Samuel and Kings first, then Chronicles.** Chronicles retells them.
  Where its Hebrew is the same as the earlier book's, Chronicles reuses the
  Samuel or Kings wording at each level; where the Hebrew differs, the wording
  differs. A rendering decision is added only where the language made its own
  choice.
- **Poems drafted once.** 2 Samuel 22 is Psalm 18, 1 Chronicles 16:8-36 is
  Psalms 105, 96 and 106, and 2 Kings 18:13-20:19 is Isaiah 36-39. None of
  those books is drafted in Hindi or Russian yet: draft them here, and the
  later books will reuse this wording. Lines (`\q`) only where the English
  level has them.
- **Genealogies (1 Chronicles 1-9)** keep one list formula per level ("the
  sons of X: A, B and C"; "X fathered Y"), as Matthew 1 does in this
  translation (Hindi उत्पन्न किया at L; Russian родил at L), and every name in
  the forms of Genesis, Numbers and this note.

## Regnal formulas

One wording per level through Kings and Chronicles.

- **Accession.** "In the Nth year of X king of Judah, Y son of Z became king
  over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned N years." Hindi L: यहूदा के राजा X
  के Nवें वर्ष में Z का पुत्र Y सामरिया में इस्राएल पर राज्य करने लगा, और उसने
  N वर्ष राज्य किया। Russian L: В N-й год X, царя Иудейского, воцарился Y,
  сын Z, над Израилем в Самарии и царствовал N лет. B smooths; R may split
  the sentence.
- **Age and mother (Judah).** Hindi: जब वह राजा बना तब N वर्ष का था, और उसने
  यरूशलेम में N वर्ष राज्य किया; उसकी माता का नाम X था। Russian: Ему было N лет,
  когда он воцарился, и N лет он царствовал в Иерусалиме; имя его матери —
  X.
- **Verdict.** "He did what was evil / right in the eyes of the LORD": Hindi
  उसने वह किया जो `{{divine_name:प्रभु|याहवे}}` की दृष्टि में बुरा / ठीक था; Russian
  он делал то, что было злом / правильным в глазах `{{divine_name:ГОСПОДА|Яхве}}`
  (L, B), что не нравилось / нравилось ГОСПОДУ at R.
- **Source.** "Are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the
  kings of Israel?": Hindi क्या वे इस्राएल के राजाओं के इतिहास की पुस्तक में
  नहीं लिखे हैं? Russian Разве это не записано в книге летописи царей Израиля?
- **Death.** "He slept with his fathers and was buried": Hindi वह अपने
  `{{gender:पूर्वजों|पुरखाओं}}` के साथ सो गया (L, B), उसकी मृत्यु हुई (R),
  where the English has a span; Russian почил со своими отцами (L, B), умер
  (R).
