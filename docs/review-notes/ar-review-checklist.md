# Arabic review checklist

The starting point for native Arabic reviewers. **This is an outline written
before drafting began**: it lists the book-wide choices the drafts follow, in
the order a reviewer should settle them, so that the most consequential ones
can be settled before many books are drafted. The counts, the recurring
uncertain decisions, the inconsistencies and the passage list are filled in
from `content/` once books are drafted (the bracketed notes say how).

Most of the Arabic wording follows a few dozen book-wide choices: the divine
name and its article rule, the forms of address and the dual, the key terms,
the recurring formulas, the name forms. A reviewer who settles those first
settles the wording of thousands of verses at once, so start with section 1.

How to use it:

- **Work down section 1 first.** Each item decides wording in many verses.
  Tick it when you agree, or write the change you want beside it.
- **Then section 2**, the uncertain decisions that recur: each cluster is one
  question asked many times; answer it once.
- **Then sections 3 and 4**: places where the drafts disagree with each
  other, and the famous passages to check against Van Dyck and the modern
  versions for originality.
- The full list of Arabic decisions marked uncertain, with links, will be
  `docs/review-notes/ar-uncertain.md`, generated by
  `python3 scripts/review_checklist.py ar --write` (do not edit it by hand).
- **Changing a choice** means editing the doc that records it (the style
  guide or the glossary; each item names the section) and then revising every
  passage drafted under it, at each level it applies to, with the rendering
  decisions that cite it. A choice is not changed by editing one verse.
- Records stay `ai_draft` until a named human reviewer approves them. Nothing
  here is marked reviewed by Claude.

Doc names below are short: "Style" is `docs/arabic-style.md`, "Terms" is
`docs/arabic-terms.md`. Levels are L (حرفية), B (متوازنة) and R (ميسّرة).

## 1. Settle these first

### God, the divine name and setting spans

- [ ] **God is الله** (إله with a suffix: إلهي، إلهك، الرب إلهك) at every
  level, with no setting. Style §4.
- [ ] **YHWH is `{{divine_name:الرب|يهوه}}`**: الرب by default, يهوه as the
  setting. Alternative: يهوه by default. Style §4. [count]
- [ ] **The article rule**: never لـ before the span (إلى الرب, not للرب);
  `يا {{divine_name:رب|يهوه}}` in address; `{{divine_name:رب|يهوه}} الجنود`.
  Style §4. [count of إلى before the span; any لل{{ left]
- [ ] **Adonai YHWH is السيّد الرب**, *السيّد* outside the span. Style §4.
- [ ] **Lord is الرب; a human master السيّد; "sir" يا سيّد.** Style §4.
- [ ] **No deity_pronoun and no christos spans**: Arabic has no capitals,
  and المسيح is both "Christ" and "Messiah". Style §4, §8.
- [ ] **Brothers**: `{{gender:الإخوة والأخوات|الإخوة}}`, inclusive by default;
  `{{gender:أبناء الله وبناته|أبناء الله}}`, `{{gender:الناس|الرجال}}`.
  Alternative: الإخوة with no span, as the Arabic Bibles. Style §5, §8.
  [count]
- [ ] **Units**: metric by default, the original unit as the alternative;
  coins as wages. Style §8.

### Register, address and grammar

- [ ] **Modern Standard Arabic at every level**, Readable included; no
  dialect, and none of Van Dyck's period forms (هوذا outside L, إصحاح,
  خطية, الناموس). Style §3. [search counts]
- [ ] **No full vocalisation**; a shadda or vowel only to prevent a
  misreading (البرّ, علّم, قُتل). Style §3.
- [ ] **The dual** wherever two people or things are meant (اذهبا, Mark
  11:2), at every level, unbracketed. Style §5.
- [ ] **Feminine forms** for women addressed as a group and for Zion and
  cities addressed as women. Style §5.
- [ ] **Address**: أنت to everyone, God included; respect by titles (يا
  سيّدي، يا معلّم، أيها الملك / يا جلالة الملك at R). "Woman": يا امرأة (L,
  B), يا سيّدتي or يا أمّي (R). Style §5.
- [ ] **"Your servant" for "I"**: عبدك / أمتك at L and B, أنا at R. Style §5.
- [ ] **Speech**: قال for everyone; a colon before the quotation; قائلًا at L
  only. Style §5.

### Key theological terms

- [ ] **Key terms**: النعمة، المجد / مجّد، الحق، البرّ / برّر، الإيمان / آمن
  بـ، الفرح، الخلاص / خلّص، الإنجيل (البشارة at R)، العالم، أبدي، آية، السرّ،
  السلام. Style §7.
- [ ] **The Law is الشريعة**, not الناموس; التوراة for the books of Moses.
  Style §7, §15. [count]
- [ ] ***hesed* is المحبة الثابتة**, keeping الرحمة for mercy. Alternative:
  الرحمة for both, as Van Dyck. In Ruth, between people: الوفاء (L, B),
  المعروف (R). Style §7, §15; Terms §2.
- [ ] **Sacrifices**: المحرقة، تقدمة الدقيق، ذبيحة السلامة، ذبيحة الخطيئة،
  ذبيحة الإثم. Terms §3.
- [ ] **Paradise الفردوس, judgment day يوم الدينونة, the age to come الدهر
  الآتي**: not the Islamic الجنّة، يوم القيامة، الآخرة. Style §9.
- [ ] **Gentiles**: الأمم (L, B), غير اليهود (R); never الكفّار. Style §9.

### Names and spelling

- [ ] **The established church forms** (يسوع، يوحنا، إيليا، يونان، أليشع،
  أخنوخ), not the Qur'anic forms (عيسى، يحيى، إلياس، يونس، اليسع، إدريس);
  the shared forms kept where they coincide (إبراهيم، موسى، داود). Style §6.
- [ ] **أورشليم, not القدس**, in the text. Style §6.
- [ ] **Book names** as the Arabic Bibles' tables of contents (التكوين ...
  الرؤيا). Style §6.
- [ ] **Forms coined while drafting** (Terms "Added while drafting"). [list
  each with its first verse]

### Punctuation, numbers and typography

- [ ] **Quotation marks « » outside, “ ” inside**, no space inside. Style §10.
- [ ] **Arabic punctuation** ، ؛ ؟. Style §10.
- [ ] **Numbers** in words where short, Western digits otherwise.
  Alternative: Arabic-Indic digits ٠–٩ across the Arabic site, verse numbers
  included. Style §10, §15.
- [ ] **Level names**: حرفية، متوازنة، ميسّرة. Style §3.

## 4. Passages to read closely

heart, or a modern version. Read each against Van Dyck and at least one
modern version (the Good News Arabic or the New Arabic Version) and make
sure no clause is copied (Style §1):

- Exodus 3:14; 20:1-17; 34:6-7
- Numbers 6:24-26; Deuteronomy 6:4-9
