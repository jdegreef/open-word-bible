# Indonesian review checklist

The starting point for native Indonesian reviewers. **This is an outline
written before drafting began**: it lists the book-wide choices the drafts
will follow, in the order a reviewer should settle them, so that review can
start as soon as the first book is drafted. The counts, the recurring
uncertain decisions, the inconsistencies and the passage list are filled in
from `content/` once books are drafted (the bracketed notes say how).

Most of the Indonesian wording will follow a few dozen book-wide choices:
the divine name and the pronouns for God, the forms of address, the key
terms, the recurring formulas, the name forms. A reviewer who settles those
first settles the wording of thousands of verses at once, so start with
section 1.

How to use it:

- **Work down section 1 first.** Each item decides wording in many verses.
  Tick it when you agree, or write the change you want beside it.
- **Then section 2**, the uncertain decisions that recur: each cluster is
  one question asked many times; answer it once.
- **Then sections 3 and 4**: places where the drafts disagree with each
  other, and the famous passages to check against the well-known Indonesian
  versions for originality.
- The full list of Indonesian decisions marked uncertain, with links, will
  be `docs/review-notes/id-uncertain.md`, generated by
  `python3 scripts/review_checklist.py id --write` (do not edit it by hand).
- **Changing a choice** means editing the doc that records it (the style
  guide or the glossary; each item names the section) and then revising
  every passage drafted under it, at each level it applies to, with the
  rendering decisions that cite it. A choice is not changed by editing one
  verse.
- Records stay `ai_draft` until a named human reviewer approves them.
  Nothing here is marked reviewed by Claude.

Doc names below are short: "Style" is `docs/indonesian-style.md`, "Terms"
is `docs/indonesian-terms.md`. Levels are L (Harfiah), B (Seimbang) and R
(Mudah Dibaca). [Counts: Balanced renderings unless stated, from `content/`
on the date the checklist is filled in.]

## 1. Settle these first

### Names of God and setting spans

- [ ] **God is *Allah*** at every level, for *theos* and *Elohim*; false
  gods *allah*, *dewa*, *dewi*; no setting. Alternatives: none proposed;
  *Elohim* (Sacred Name usage) is not used. Style §4. [count]
- [ ] **YHWH is `{{divine_name:TUHAN|Yahweh}}`** at every level, in
  capitals, nothing inside the span but the name. Alternatives: *Yahwe* as
  the setting's spelling; *Yahweh* as the default. Style §4; Terms §1.
  [count, books]
- [ ] **"Lord GOD" (*Adonai YHWH*) is `Tuhan {{divine_name:ALLAH|Yahweh}}`**;
  *YHWH Elohim* is `{{divine_name:TUHAN|Yahweh}} Allah`; *YHWH tsevaot* is
  `{{divine_name:TUHAN|Yahweh}} semesta alam`. Alternatives: *Tuhan TUHAN*;
  *TUHAN balatentara*. Style §4. [count; about 200 in Ezekiel]
- [ ] **Lord is *Tuhan*; a human master *tuan*; "sir" *Tuan*.** Style §4.
- [ ] **Pronouns for God under `deity_pronoun`**: *Dia*, *-Nya*, *Engkau*,
  *-Mu*, *Kau-*, *Aku*, *-Ku*, *Kita* (Genesis 1:26), capitalised by default;
  *Dia*, never *Ia*, for God. The lowercase option reads *firman-nya* with a
  hyphen. Alternatives: *Ia* as subject; a validator change so lowercase
  reads *firmannya*. Style §4. [count]
- [ ] **Who else takes a span**: the angel of the LORD speaking as God, the
  king of Psalms 45 and 110, the servant of Matthew 12:18, as in the
  English; angels as messengers, the servant of Isaiah, "one like a son of
  man" do not. Style §4. [uncertain decisions that turn on it]
- [ ] **Christ under `christos`**: `{{christos:Kristus|Mesias}}`; with the
  name `Yesus {{christos:Kristus|Sang Mesias}}` (B, R), `Yesus
  {{christos:Kristus|Mesias}}` (L); *Mesias* plainly at John 1:41 and 4:25.
  Alternative: *Mesias* as the default. Style §8. [count]
- [ ] **Gender spans are rare.** *saudara-saudara* takes none; spans only on
  *anēr* in general (`{{gender:orang|laki-laki}}`), *huioi* meaning children
  (`{{gender:anak-anak|anak-anak laki-laki}}`) and ancestors
  (`{{gender:nenek moyang|bapa leluhur}}`). Alternative:
  `{{gender:saudara-saudari|saudara-saudara}}` for believers. Style §5, §8.
- [ ] **Units under `units`**: metric by default at B and R, the original
  unit as the alternative, Literal the original unit only; cubit 45 cm
  (Ezekiel 40–48: 53 cm); coins as wages. Style §8; Terms §6. [count]

### Address forms and speech

- [ ] **Plural "you" is *kalian*** at every level, *kalian* as possessor
  (*Bapa kalian*); no `plural_you` setting. Alternatives: *kamu* (L);
  *kamu sekalian* in speeches. Style §5. [count]
- [ ] **God and Jesus to one person**: *engkau*, *-mu*, *kau-* (L, B);
  *kamu* (R). Style §5.
- [ ] **Everyone to God and Jesus**: *Engkau*, *-Mu*, *Kau-* with the span,
  disciples and opponents alike. Style §5.
- [ ] **"I"**: God and Jesus *Aku*; people *aku* to equals, family and in
  prayer, *saya* to a superior or a stranger; *hamba* as "I" only at L.
  Style §5. [uncertain register decisions]
- [ ] **Letter writers** say *aku* (L, B) and *saya* (R). Alternative: one
  form at every level. Style §5.
- [ ] **Inclusive *kita*, exclusive *kami*.** Genesis 1:26 *Kita*; John 1:14
  and 1 John 1:1 *kami*; Paul with his readers *kita*, Paul and his
  companions *kami*. Style §5. [uncertain decisions]
- [ ] **"Woman" in address** is *Perempuan* (L) and *Ibu* (B, R), Jesus's
  mother included. Style §5.
- [ ] **Kings**: subjects say *Tuanku* (L, B), *Baginda* or *Raja* (R).
  Style §5, §12. [count]
- [ ] **Brothers and sisters**: *Saudara-saudaraku* (L, B),
  *Saudara-saudara* (R); "beloved" *yang kukasihi* (L, B), *yang saya
  kasihi* (R). Style §5.
- [ ] **Verbs of speaking**: *berfirman* for God, *berkata* for Jesus and
  everyone else; never *bersabda*. Style §5.
- [ ] **No *Anda*** in the Bible text. Style §5.

### Key theological terms

- [ ] **The sixteen key terms**: *kasih karunia*, *kemuliaan*,
  *memuliakan*, *kebenaran* (truth), *kebenaran* (righteousness), *iman*,
  *percaya*, *sukacita*, *keselamatan*, *Injil* / *kabar baik* (R), *dunia*,
  *kekal*, *tanda*, *rahasia*, *damai sejahtera* / *damai* (R), YHWH as
  above. Style §7.
- [ ] **Truth and righteousness share *kebenaran*.** Alternatives: *keadilan*
  for *dikaiosynē* throughout; a distinct word for truth. Style §7; Terms §2.
  [count of records with both words]
- [ ] **Grace is *kasih karunia*, not *anugerah* or *rahmat*.** Style §7.
- [ ] ***hesed* is *kasih setia*** at every level. Terms §2.
- [ ] **Law of Moses is *hukum Taurat*.** Terms §2.
- [ ] **Sacrifice is *korban*, never *kurban*; *shelamim* is *korban
  persekutuan*** (uncertain; alternative *korban keselamatan*). Terms §3.
- [ ] ***doulos* / *ʿeved*: *budak* (L), *hamba* (B, R)**, *budak* where
  slavery is the point. Style §7; Terms §5.
- [ ] **Advocate (*paraklētos*) is *Pembela***; alternatives *Penolong*,
  *Penghibur*. Terms §1.
- [ ] **Demons and the devil**: *setan* (L) / *roh jahat* (B, R) for
  demons; *Iblis* for *diabolos*; *Satan* for *satanas*. Style §7.
- [ ] **Gentiles** are *bangsa-bangsa lain* / *orang bukan Yahudi*, never
  *orang kafir*. Style §9.

### Recurring formulas

- [ ] *Beginilah firman TUHAN* (L, B), *Inilah firman TUHAN* (R); *firman
  TUHAN* / *demikianlah firman TUHAN* / *itulah firman TUHAN* for *ne'um
  YHWH*. Style §12a. [count]
- [ ] Truly I tell you: *Amin, Aku berkata kepada kalian* (L), *Sesungguhnya
  Aku berkata kepada kalian* (B), *Aku mengatakan yang sebenarnya kepada
  kalian* (R). Style §7.
- [ ] *hinneh* / *idou*: *lihatlah* (L), *lihat* or left out (B), left out
  or *ketahuilah* (R). Style §12a.
- [ ] *wayehi*: *dan terjadilah* (L), left out or a time clause (B, R).
- [ ] The regnal formulas of Kings and Chronicles. Terms §7.
- [ ] Psalm titles: *Untuk pemimpin musik*; *Mazmur Daud*; *Nyanyian
  ziarah*; *Sela*. Terms §4.

### Names and spelling

- [ ] **Established church forms** (*Yesus*, *Musa*, *Daud*, *Salomo*,
  *Yohanes*, *Petrus*, *Yakub* / *Yakobus*), church forms over Islamic forms
  where they differ (*Abraham*, not *Ibrahim*). Style §6; Terms §8–11.
- [ ] **Forms coined while drafting** (Terms §13). [list each with its
  first verse]
- [ ] **Spelling**: EYD and KBBI (*mukjizat*, *napas*), not TB's older
  spellings. Style §10.
- [ ] **Numbers**: one or two words in words, others in digits; thousands
  with a full stop. Style §10.
- [ ] **Quotation marks** straight in the data, curled by the site; comma
  before a quotation. Style §5, §10.

### Style

- [ ] **Register**: no Classical Malay (*maka*, *hatta*, *syahdan*,
  *bahwasanya*, *tatkala*, *beroleh*) and no Jakarta colloquial (*nggak*,
  *banget*, *aja*). Style §3. [search counts]
- [ ] **Arabic-derived vocabulary**: church vocabulary kept; Islamic
  technical terms (*salat*, *pahala*, *kiamat*, *akhirat*, *Isa*) not used.
  Style §9. [search counts]
- [ ] **Readable affixes and reduplication**: plain verbs, no *banyak
  orang-orang*, natural passives. Style §11.
- [ ] **Level names**: *Harfiah*, *Seimbang*, *Mudah Dibaca*. Style §3.
