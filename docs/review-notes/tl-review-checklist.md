# Tagalog review checklist

The starting point for native Tagalog reviewers. **This is an outline
written before drafting began**: it lists the book-wide choices the drafts
will follow, in the order a reviewer should settle them, so that review can
start as soon as the first book is drafted. The counts, the recurring
uncertain decisions, the inconsistencies and the passage list are filled in
from `content/` once books are drafted (the bracketed notes say how).

Most of the Tagalog wording will follow a few dozen book-wide choices: the
divine name and the markers inside its span, the pronouns for God, the
forms of address and *po*, the key terms, the recurring formulas, the name
forms. A reviewer who settles those first settles the wording of thousands
of verses at once, so start with section 1.

How to use it:

- **Work down section 1 first.** Each item decides wording in many verses.
  Tick it when you agree, or write the change you want beside it.
- **Then section 2**, the uncertain decisions that recur: each cluster is
  one question asked many times; answer it once.
- **Then sections 3 and 4**: places where the drafts disagree with each
  other, and the famous passages to check against the well-known Tagalog
  versions for originality.
- The full list of Tagalog decisions marked uncertain, with links, will be
  `docs/review-notes/tl-uncertain.md`, generated by
  `python3 scripts/review_checklist.py tl --write` (do not edit it by hand).
- **Changing a choice** means editing the doc that records it (the style
  guide or the glossary; each item names the section) and then revising
  every passage drafted under it, at each level it applies to, with the
  rendering decisions that cite it. A choice is not changed by editing one
  verse.
- Records stay `ai_draft` until a named human reviewer approves them.
  Nothing here is marked reviewed by Claude.

Doc names below are short: "Style" is `docs/tagalog-style.md`, "Terms" is
`docs/tagalog-terms.md`. Levels are L (Literal), B (Balanse) and R
(Madaling Basahin). [Counts: Balanced renderings unless stated, from
`content/` on the date the checklist is filled in.]

## 1. Settle these first

### Names of God and setting spans

- [ ] **God is *Diyos*** at every level, for *theos* and *Elohim*; false
  gods *diyos*, *diyosa*; no setting; never *Dios* or *Bathala*. Style §4.
- [ ] **YHWH is *ang PANGINOON*, with *si Yahweh* as the setting**, the case
  marker inside the span (`{{divine_name:ang PANGINOON|si Yahweh}}`,
  `ng PANGINOON|ni Yahweh`, `sa PANGINOON|kay Yahweh`) and a following
  linker too (`ang PANGINOONG|si Yahweh na`). Alternatives: *Yahweh* as
  the default (as MBB); *Jehova* is not used. Style §4; Terms §1. [count,
  books]
- [ ] **"Lord GOD" (*Adonai YHWH*) is `Panginoong {{divine_name:DIYOS|Yahweh}}`**;
  *YHWH Elohim* is `ang PANGINOONG Diyos` / `si Yahweh na Diyos`; *YHWH
  tsevaot* is `ang PANGINOON ng mga hukbo`. Alternative: *Makapangyarihan
  sa Lahat* for *tsevaot* at every level. Style §4. [count; about 200 in
  Ezekiel]
- [ ] **Lord is *Panginoon*; a human master *panginoon* (L, B) or *amo*
  (R); "sir" *Ginoo*.** Style §4.
- [ ] **Pronouns for God under `deity_pronoun`**: *Siya*, *Niya*,
  *Kaniya*, *Kaniyang*, *Ikaw*, *Ka*, *Mo*, *Iyo*, *Iyong*, capitalised by
  default, exactly where the English has a span; none on *ako*, *ko*,
  *akin*. Alternatives: no spans at all (as Tagalog Bibles print it);
  spans on the first person too. Style §4. [count]
- [ ] **Who else takes a span**: as the English (the angel of the LORD
  speaking as God, the king of Psalms 45 and 110). Style §4.
- [ ] **Christ under `christos`**, marker inside the span:
  `{{christos:si Cristo|ang Mesiyas}}`, `kay Cristo|sa Mesiyas`,
  `si Jesus {{christos:Cristo|na Mesiyas}}`; *Jesu-Cristo* not used.
  Alternative: *ang Cristo* (title) as the default everywhere. Style §8.
  [count]
- [ ] **Gender spans are rare.** *mga kapatid* takes none; spans on *anēr*
  in general, *huioi* meaning children and "fathers" as ancestors. Style
  §5, §8.
- [ ] **Units under `units`**: metric by default at B and R, the original
  unit as the alternative, Literal the original unit only; cubit 45 cm;
  coins as wages. Style §8; Terms §6. [count]

### Address forms and speech

- [ ] **"You" to God and Jesus is *ikaw*, *mo***, from everyone, with the
  span; no respectful plural (*kayo*, *ninyo* to one person) anywhere in
  the text. Alternative: *kayo* to Jesus from petitioners (as ASND). Style
  §5. [count]
- [ ] ***Po* and *opo*** at B and R for speech to superiors, elders, kings,
  priests, prophets and from petitioners to Jesus; never at L, never in
  prayer to God, never from Jesus. Alternatives: *po* in prayer at R; no
  *po* at B. Style §5. [count of records with *po*]
- [ ] **Inclusive *tayo*, exclusive *kami*.** John 1:14 and 1 John 1:1
  *kami*; Paul with his readers *tayo*; a people praying *kami*. Style §5.
  [uncertain decisions]
- [ ] **"Woman" in address** is *Babae* (L), *Ginang* (B, R), *Ina* for
  Jesus's mother at R. Style §5.
- [ ] **Kings**: *panginoon kong hari* (L), *Mahal na Hari* (B, R); "your
  servant" as self-reference only at L. Style §5, §12. [count]
- [ ] **Brothers and sisters**: *mga kapatid*, no gender span. Style §5.

### Key theological terms

- [ ] **The key terms**: *biyaya*, *kaluwalhatian*, *luwalhatiin*,
  *katotohanan*, *katuwiran*, *pananampalataya*, *sumampalataya*,
  *kagalakan*, *kaligtasan*, *Ebanghelyo* / *Magandang Balita* (R),
  *sanlibutan*, *walang hanggan*, *tanda*, *hiwaga*, *kapayapaan*, *tapat
  na pag-ibig* (*hesed*), *tipan*, *Kautusan*. Style §7.
- [ ] **World is *sanlibutan*, not *mundo*.** Style §7.
- [ ] **Justify is *ariing-matuwid*** (L, B), *ituring na matuwid* (R);
  alternative ADB's *ariing-ganap*. Style §7.
- [ ] **Holy Spirit is *Banal na Espiritu***; alternative *Espiritu Santo*.
  Style §7, §14.
- [ ] **Baptize is *bautismuhan***, *Juan na Tagapagbautismo*; alternatives
  *binyagan*, *Juan Bautista*. Style §7, §14.
- [ ] **Priest is *pari***, high priest *punong pari*; alternative
  *saserdote*. Style §7.
- [ ] ***shelamim* is *handog para sa pakikipag-isa***; alternative *handog
  pangkapayapaan*. Terms §3.
- [ ] ***doulos* / *ʿeved***: *alipin* where slavery is the point, *lingkod*
  for a servant, by the English at each level. Style §7; Terms §5.
- [ ] **Gentiles**: *mga bansa* (L), *mga Hentil* (B), *mga hindi Judio*
  (R). Style §7.

### Recurring formulas

- [ ] *Ganito ang sabi ng PANGINOON* (L, B), *Ito ang sinasabi ng
  PANGINOON* (R); *sabi ng PANGINOON* / *ang sabi ng PANGINOON* for
  *ne'um YHWH*. Style §12a. [count]
- [ ] Truly I tell you: *Amen, sinasabi ko sa inyo* (L), *Totoo ang
  sinasabi ko sa inyo* (B), *Tandaan ninyo ito* (R). Style §7.
- [ ] *hinneh* / *idou*: *narito* (L), *tingnan ninyo* or left out (B),
  left out (R). Style §12a.
- [ ] *wayehi*: *At nangyari* (L), left out or a time clause (B, R).
- [ ] Psalm titles: *Para sa punong mang-aawit*; *Awit ni David*; *awit ng
  pag-akyat*; *Sela*. Terms §4.

### Names and spelling

- [ ] **Spanish-derived church forms without accents** (*Jesus*, *Juan*,
  *Pedro*, *Santiago*, *Moises*, *Josue*, *Isaias*, *Jose*, *Noe*). Style
  §6; Terms §8–11.
- [ ] **Forms coined while drafting** (Terms §13). [list each with its
  first verse]
- [ ] **Spelling**: Ortograpiyang Pambansa (*Diyos*, *iglesya*, *kaniya*,
  *sapagkat*); *ng* / *nang*; *din* / *rin*. Style §10. [search counts]
- [ ] **Contractions**: only *ako'y*-type at L and B and in poetry; none at
  R. Style §10.
- [ ] **Numbers** in Tagalog words; digits for long numbers with a comma
  for thousands; no Spanish number words. Style §10.

### Style

- [ ] **Register**: no ADB Tagalog (*datapuwa't*, *anopa't*, *yaon*, the
  *nangag-* plurals) and no Taglish or street forms (*nung*, *'yung*,
  *sobra*, *grabe*). Style §3. [search counts]
- [ ] **Catholic and Protestant usage**: shared vocabulary, no
  denominational names, *Maria* without titles. Style §9.
- [ ] **Focus and aspect**: natural Tagalog focus at every level; no
  bracketing of grammar at L. Style §11.
- [ ] **Level names**: *Literal*, *Balanse*, *Madaling Basahin*. Style §3.
- [ ] **Language name**: *Tagalog* (alternative *Filipino*) in the
  language switch. Style §14.

## Open questions from drafting

- **Ruth 4:1, "Mr. So-and-so"**: drafted as *kaibigan* (friend) at every
  level, since the placeholder names *kuwan* and *si Ano* sound colloquial
  or comic. A reviewer should confirm or suggest a better form (r-tl-1 in
  RUT.4.1.s1).
