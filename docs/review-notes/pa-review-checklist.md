# Punjabi review checklist

The starting point for native Punjabi reviewers. **This is an outline
written before drafting began**: it lists the book-wide choices the drafts
follow, in the order a reviewer should settle them, so that review can start
as soon as the first book is drafted. The counts, the recurring uncertain
decisions, the inconsistencies and the passage list are filled in from
`content/` once books are drafted (the bracketed notes say how).

Most of the Punjabi wording follows a few dozen book-wide choices: the
divine name, the honorifics and forms of address, the key terms, the words
kept clear of Sikh and Hindu theology, the formulas and the name forms. A
reviewer who settles those first settles the wording of thousands of verses
at once, so start with section 1.

How to use it:

- **Work down section 1 first.** Each item decides wording in many verses.
  Tick it when you agree, or write the change you want beside it.
- **Then section 2**, the uncertain decisions that recur: each cluster is
  one question asked many times; answer it once.
- **Then sections 3 and 4**: places where the drafts disagree with each
  other, and the famous passages to check against the 1945 Bible and the IRV
  for originality.
- The full list of Punjabi decisions marked uncertain, with links, will be
  `docs/review-notes/pa-uncertain.md`, generated by
  `python3 scripts/review_checklist.py pa --write` (do not edit it by hand).
- **Changing a choice** means editing the doc that records it (the style
  guide or the glossary; each item names the section) and then revising
  every passage drafted under it, at each level it applies to, with the
  rendering decisions that cite it. A choice is not changed by editing one
  verse.
- Records stay `ai_draft` until a named human reviewer approves them.
  Nothing here is marked reviewed by Claude.

Doc names below are short: "Style" is `docs/punjabi-style.md`, "Terms" is
`docs/punjabi-terms.md`. Levels are L (ਸ਼ਾਬਦਿਕ), B (ਸੰਤੁਲਿਤ) and R (ਸਰਲ).

## 1. Settle these first

### Script and register

- [ ] **Gurmukhi, standard Indian Punjabi** at every level, no dialect
  forms, no Hindi or Urdu grammar. Shahmukhi (`pnb`) is a later, separate
  edition. Style §3, §15.
- [ ] **Modern standard spelling**: ਪੁੱਤਰ, ਪਵਿੱਤਰ, not the 1945 Bible's
  ਪੁੱਤ੍ਰ, ਪਵਿੱਤ੍ਰ; nukta where dictionaries write it (ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਖ਼ਬਰੀ, ਜ਼ਮੀਨ);
  tippi and bindi by rule. Style §10. [search counts of pairin-ਰ forms]
- [ ] **Danda । as the full stop**, straight quotes in records,
  international digits, Indian digit grouping. Style §10.

### Names of God and setting spans

- [ ] **God is ਪਰਮੇਸ਼ੁਰ** at every level; not ਰੱਬ, ਖ਼ੁਦਾ, ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ, ਈਸ਼ਵਰ,
  ਭਗਵਾਨ. Alternative: ਰੱਬ at Readable. Style §4.
- [ ] **YHWH is `{{divine_name:ਪ੍ਰਭੂ|ਯਾਹਵੇ}}`**, postpositions outside the
  span; ਯਹੋਵਾਹ (both Punjabi Bibles) is not used. Alternative: ਯਹੋਵਾਹ as
  the default. Style §4; Terms §1. [count, books]
- [ ] ***Adonai YHWH* is `ਪ੍ਰਭੂ {{divine_name:ਪਰਮੇਸ਼ੁਰ|ਯਾਹਵੇ}}`**; *YHWH
  tsevaot* `ਸੈਨਾਂ ਦਾ {{divine_name:ਪ੍ਰਭੂ|ਯਾਹਵੇ}}`. Style §4.
- [ ] **No `deity_pronoun` and no `christos` spans**: Gurmukhi has no
  capitals, and ਮਸੀਹ is both Christ and Messiah. Style §8.
- [ ] **Gender spans are fewer than the English**: brothers and sisters,
  *anēr* meaning people, "sons" meaning children; ਲੋਕ, ਮਨੁੱਖ, ਕੋਈ, ਸਭ take
  none. Style §5, §8.
- [ ] **Units**: metric by default, the original unit as the alternative;
  Literal the original unit; coins as wages. Style §8; Terms §6. [count]

### Address and honorifics

- [ ] **Honorific plural for God and Jesus** in the narrative (ਯਿਸੂ ਆਏ,
  ਉਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਨੇ ਕਿਹਾ); plain singular when opponents speak of Jesus and when
  Jesus speaks of the Son of Man. Style §5. [uncertain decisions]
- [ ] **ਤੂੰ to God** in prayer and psalms; **ਤੁਸੀਂ to Jesus** from everyone.
  Style §5.
- [ ] **Jesus and God say ਤੂੰ to one person, ਤੁਸੀਂ to many.** Alternative:
  ਤੁਸੀਂ to everyone, as polite modern speech. Style §5.
- [ ] **Within families and among equals ਤੂੰ; to elders, in-laws and rulers
  ਤੁਸੀਂ**; kings ਹੇ ਮਹਾਰਾਜ (B, R). Style §5.

### Words kept clear of other theologies

- [ ] **Not used**: ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ, ਅਕਾਲ ਪੁਰਖ, ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ, ਗੁਰਬਾਣੀ, ਗੁਰਦੁਆਰਾ, ਸੰਗਤ,
  ਗ੍ਰੰਥੀ, ਅਰਦਾਸ, ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ, ਸੰਤ; ਭਗਵਾਨ, ਈਸ਼ਵਰ, ਕਰਮ, ਮੋਕਸ਼; ਖ਼ੁਦਾ, ਪੈਗੰਬਰ, ਜੰਨਤ.
  Style §9. [search counts; should be 0]
- [ ] **ਗੁਰੂ for "Teacher"** (Rabbi stays ਰੱਬੀ). Alternative: ਉਸਤਾਦ. Style
  §9, §15.
- [ ] **ਸ਼ਬਦ for the Logos** (John 1). Alternative: ਬਚਨ. Style §9, §15.
- [ ] **ਮੁਕਤੀ for salvation**, the word of both Punjabi Bibles, despite its
  Sikh and Hindu sense of liberation. Style §7.

### Key theological terms

- [ ] **The key terms**: ਕਿਰਪਾ, ਦਯਾ, ਅਟੱਲ ਪਿਆਰ (*hesed*), ਮਹਿਮਾ, ਸਚਾਈ / ਸੱਚ
  (R), ਧਾਰਮਿਕਤਾ, ਧਰਮੀ ਠਹਿਰਾਉਣਾ, ਵਿਸ਼ਵਾਸ (not ਨਿਹਚਾ), ਤੋਬਾ, ਨੇਮ, ਬਿਵਸਥਾ,
  ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਖ਼ਬਰੀ, ਬਪਤਿਸਮਾ, ਕਲੀਸਿਯਾ, ਸੰਸਾਰ, ਸਦੀਪਕ ਜੀਵਨ. Style §7; Terms §2.
- [ ] **Scribes** ਸ਼ਾਸਤਰੀ (L), ਬਿਵਸਥਾ ਦੇ ਸਿੱਖਿਅਕ (B, R). Style §7.
- [ ] **Gentiles** ਪਰਾਈਆਂ ਕੌਮਾਂ (L, B), ਗ਼ੈਰ-ਯਹੂਦੀ (R); never ਜਾਤ (caste).
  Style §7.
- [ ] **Sacrifices**: ਹੋਮ ਬਲੀ, ਪਾਪ ਬਲੀ, ਦੋਸ਼ ਬਲੀ, ਸੁੱਖ-ਸਾਂਦ ਦੀ ਬਲੀ;
  ਪ੍ਰਾਸਚਿਤ. Terms §3.

### Recurring formulas

- [ ] Thus says the LORD; declares the LORD; the word of the LORD came; I am
  the LORD. Style §12a.
- [ ] Truly I tell you: ਮੈਂ ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ ਸੱਚ ਆਖਦਾ ਹਾਂ, every level. Style §7.
- [ ] *hinneh* / *idou*: ਵੇਖੋ (L), left out (B, R). Style §12a.
- [ ] Psalm titles: ਦਾਊਦ ਦਾ ਭਜਨ; ਸਲਹ. Style §12.

### Names

- [ ] **Established Punjabi Bible forms** where the 1945 Bible and the IRV
  agree, the IRV's spelling where they differ. Style §6; Terms §8–11.
- [ ] **Forms chosen or coined while drafting** (Terms §13). [list each with

- [ ] **Level names**: ਸ਼ਾਬਦਿਕ, ਸੰਤੁਲਿਤ, ਸਰਲ; language name ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.
