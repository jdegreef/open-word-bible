# Where a verse and its own footnote disagree

Translating the footnotes into eleven languages (Spanish, French, Portuguese,
Vietnamese, Swahili, Korean, Russian, Hindi, Amharic, Japanese and Ukrainian)
meant reading every footnoted meaning decision next to the verse it explains.
The translators listed the places where the verse text, in Balanced or Readable,
says something the decision's choice does not: usually it follows the reading
the footnote gives as the alternative.

None of these renderings was changed. Each one needs a decision, and most need
it once, in English, before the other languages follow.

Three groups:

1. **The English text already departs from the note.** The languages copied the
   English. Decide in English first: either the Balanced and Readable wording is
   an acceptable way to say the chosen sense (then nothing changes, and the
   footnote may need a sentence saying so), or the wording follows the reading
   the decision set aside (then English and every language change together).
2. **Only some languages depart.** The English matches its note; the listed
   languages do not. Fix them in those languages.
3. **Errors in the text or in the English notes.** Spelling and typing slips,
   and English notes that are wrong or contradict each other.

"Choice" is the meaning decision's choice; L, B and R are the Literal, Balanced
and Readable levels.

## 1. The English text departs from the note

Ordered by how many languages the translators flagged. The meaning decisions
are shared, so the same question stands in every language whether flagged or not.

| Verse | Choice | English B | Languages flagged |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luke 8:48, 17:19, 18:42 | has saved you: made well, and more than that, rescued | "has made you well" | ru, am, ja |
| Joel 4:21 | I will hold innocent their blood (vindicate) | "I will avenge their blood"; R "I will punish", the Greek reading | pt, hi, ru, am, uk |
| 2 Kings 9:32 | eunuchs | "officials" | pt, ko, hi, am, ja |
| Micah 6:9 | sound wisdom will see your name | "the wise will respect your name", near the alternative "fear" | pt, sw, am, uk |
| Proverbs 17:27 | noble (precious) of spirit, the scribes' reading | "a steady spirit", the written form ("cool") | pt, am, ja |
| 2 Samuel 6:21 | "Before the LORD ..." left unfinished | "It was before the LORD that I danced", the Greek fill-in | hi, uk, ja |
| Philippians 3:1 | as for the rest: a turn to the next matter | "Finally" at L and B | ru, ja |
| 2 Peter 1:1, Jude 1 | slave: owned by a master | "servant" | sw, uk |
| Ecclesiastes (whole book) | Qoheleth: a title used as a name | "the Teacher" | pt, ja (ru and uk verse texts also say "Teacher") |
| Deuteronomy 32:26 | cut them in pieces (perhaps by scattering) | "I will scatter them" | hi, am |
| Genesis 37:36 | Medanites (the Hebrew text) or Midianites | "the Midianites" | ko, hi |
| Psalm 139:11 | crush me (the verb in the text) | "overwhelm me" | am, uk ("swallow") |
| Jeremiah 38:7 | eunuch (the note allows either) | "a court officer" | pt, ja |
| Song of Songs 8:14 | flee | "Hurry" | ko |
| Zechariah 8:3 | City of Truth | "the Faithful City" (the note says the word means both) | ko, uk |
| Ezekiel 29:7 | made their hips stand rigid (the Hebrew) | "wrenched all their hips", near the alternative | am |

Some of these are probably fine as level choices: Balanced may say "officials"
for a word whose sense is "palace officials, probably castrated", or "scatter"
where the sense is "destroy completely, perhaps by scattering". Others change
the reading:

- **Joel 4:21:** B and R follow the Greek, which the decision rejects.
- **Proverbs 17:27:** B follows the written form, where the decision chooses the scribes' reading.
- **Philippians 3:1:** L itself says "Finally", against its own note.
- **2 Peter 1:1 and Jude 1:** the note defines the word as "owned by a master", and "servant" drops that.

Those need a decision first.

## 2. Only some languages depart

The English follows its note here; these languages do not.

**Shared by two or more languages:**

| Verse | Choice (and English) | Languages and their reading |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 20:13 | murder | pt "Não matarás", am "አትግደል" (kill) |
| Song of Songs 2:1 | crocus | hi narcissus, sw "waridi-mwitu" (wild rose; also Isaiah 35:1) |
| 1 Samuel 27:8 | Gizrites (margin reading) | ru, hi: Girzites (the written form) |
| 1 Samuel 4:7 | a god has come | sw "Mungu", uk «Бог» (God) |
| 1 Samuel 8:9 | the way of the king | pt "o direito do rei", uk «права царя» (rights) |
| Leviticus 3:1 | peace offering | am, ja: fellowship offering |
| Deuteronomy 34:6 | he buried him (subject unstated) | ko, hi ("they"), ja (passive) |
| 2 Kings 23:36 | Zebudah (margin reading) | ko, am: Zebidah (the written form) |
| Luke 21:19 | gain: a command | am, ja: a future or a promise |
| Isaiah 15:7 | the Wadi of the Poplars | hi, am: willows (the alternative) |
| Isaiah 41:19 | cedar, acacia, myrtle, wild olive, juniper, elm, box | sw, hi, ja name other trees |
| Jeremiah 38:23 | you will burn this city | am, uk: passive, "this city will be burned" |

**One language:**

- **Spanish:** 1 Chronicles 4, "Birzavit" in the text where the note reads Birzait.
- **Portuguese:**
  - **Different readings:** Genesis 37:28 (makes the Midianites the subject); Ezra 8:17 ("sent" for "commanded"); Jeremiah 48:13 ("Betel, o deus").
  - **Different word chosen:**
    - Exodus 30:34 (storax for stacte); 1 Samuel 16:12 (red-haired for ruddy); 2 Kings 20:12 (Merodach for Berodach); Job 38:1 (whirlwind for storm).
    - 1 Kings 14:10 and 2 Kings 9:8 ("slave or free"); 2 Kings 20:1 and Isaiah 38:1 ("set your house in order").
    - Deuteronomy 33:17 (no "his"); Nehemiah 3:6 and 12:39 ("Porta Velha" for the name Jeshanah).
- **Vietnamese:** Acts 21:4 (the plea made Spirit-moved); Romans 10:4 ("end" only, where the note asks for "goal" too); Ezekiel 1:4, 1:27 and 8:2 at L ("amber", where the note says the substance is unknown).
- **Swahili:** Galatians 6:13 (perfect "were circumcised"); Ruth 3:9 ("corner of the garment" for "wing"); 1 Kings 10:11–12 (sandalwood for almug); Zechariah 9:15 ("rule" for "devour"); and plants, stones and details in 1 Samuel 30:17, 2 Kings 4:39, Proverbs 8:22, Isaiah 32:6, 44:14, 54:12, Jeremiah 2:16, Ezekiel 27:6 and Nahum 2:4.
- **Korean:** 1 Kings 22:6 and 2 Chronicles 18:5 (adds "그 성"); Acts 10:46 and 19:6 (the same phrase worded two ways); and Acts 13:33, John 19:26, Romans 15:23–28 (a name spelling), James 3:12, Hebrews 13:20, Exodus 18:6 and Judges 9:44 (see the Korean footnote PR).
- **Russian:** 2 Samuel 18:14 ("darts" for "rods"); 2 Samuel 21:6 ("hang", minor).
- **Hindi:**
  - **Different readings:** John 10:29 (the alternative reading; see its decision m2); Joshua 18:24 (Kephar-ammoni); Deuteronomy 33:3 ("nations"); 2 Chronicles 33:16 ("set in order", the written form).
  - **Different word chosen:** Exodus 26:15 and 36:20 ("boards" for "frames"); Ezekiel 1:16 (turquoise for chrysolite).
  - **Minor:** bird names in Leviticus 11:18 and Deuteronomy 14:16–17; Malachi 2:16 (first person); Joshua 3:16.
- **Amharic:**
  - **Different readings:** **Romans 16:7 names Julia where the note chooses Junia**; Mark 12:29 ("the Lord our God is one Lord"); 1 Kings 15:1 (Abijah for Abijam); 2 Kings 14:2 (Jehoaddan); 1 Chronicles 7:31 (Birzavith); Ezekiel 44:7 ("you broke").
  - **Different word chosen:** Hosea 13:5 ("cared for", the Greek); Psalm 68:24 ("dip" for "shatter"); Isaiah 38:14 (stork for crane).
- **Japanese:**
  - **Different readings:** Genesis 25:22; Zechariah 6:13 (the priest on the king's throne); Isaiah 32:6 ("plans" for "does"); Jeremiah 46:17 (a description, not a nickname).
  - **Wording:** Matthew 6:11 and Luke 11:3 (the bread phrase mixes the choice and its alternative); 1 Peter 5:13 ("chosen lady"); Leviticus 23:24 (names the ram's horn where the note says the instrument is not named).
- **Ukrainian:**
  - **Different readings:** Acts 18:7 (Titus Justus); Leviticus 26:34 ("make up for"); Deuteronomy 4:37 (plural); Song of Songs 6:11 (the verb makes the woman the speaker); Jeremiah 23:36 and 48:13 (commit to one reading where the note leaves it open).
  - **Different word chosen:** Genesis 2:12 ("fragrant resin" for bdellium); Genesis 28:12 (ladder for stairway); Deuteronomy 33:17.

## 3. Errors in the text and the English notes

**English notes:**
- **Leviticus 16:8:** calls 1 Enoch "not part of Scripture". It is canonical in the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, which most Amharic readers belong to. "Not in the Hebrew Bible or most Christian canons" is accurate for everyone.
- **2 Samuel 15:28 and 17:16:** describe the same "fords"/"plains" pair (written form and margin reading) the opposite way round.
- **Isaiah 9:5 (m5), 24:16 and 65:4:** an Isaiah reference follows another book's reference with no book name, for example "(Deuteronomy 32:4; 45:21)", which reads as Deuteronomy 45:21.

**Verse text:**
- **Russian:** "во моё имя" in 23 places (fixed in a separate PR); Gershon is «Гирсон» in 1 Chronicles 5:27 but «Гирсам» in 6:1.
- **Ukrainian:**
  - Gershon is «Гершон» in 1 Chronicles 5:27 but «Гершом» in 6:1.
  - Bethel is «Бетель» in Judges, Joshua, Samuel and Kings but «Бет-Ел» in Genesis, Amos and Hosea.
  - «Ліван» (2 Kings 19:23, 1 Kings, Jeremiah 18:14 and 22:6, 20, 23) and «Віфлеєм» (Jeremiah 41:17) against the settled «Ливан», «Вифлеєм».
  - «шеклів» in Deuteronomy 22:29.
- **Swahili:** Micah 6:13 repeats the pronoun ("mimi nami"); Matthew 24:16 and Romans 15:31 say "Yudea" where the text elsewhere says "Uyahudi".
- **Spanish:** Nebuchadnezzar is "Nabucodorosor" in Jeremiah and "Nabucodonosor" in Daniel.

**Name forms in the notes to settle:**
- **Japanese:** the mina is マネ in the Old Testament and ミナ in Ezra, Nehemiah and the Luke parable, each as its verse text has it.
- **Ukrainian:** Joseph is «Йосиф» in the later notes and «Йосип» in some earlier New Testament notes.
- **French:** Joshua the high priest is "Josué" in the Zechariah notes and "Yéshoua" in a Daniel note.
