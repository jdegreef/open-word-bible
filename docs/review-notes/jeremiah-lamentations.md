# Jeremiah and Lamentations: drafting and review notes

Working notes from drafting Jeremiah and Lamentations in English (merged in
#321 and #324). One line per chapter from the drafter's report, then the
consistency reviewers' follow-ups. **REVIEW** marks items that needed a
decision; **ALIGN** marks wording that had to match another passage. Most were
settled during review (see docs/jeremiah-lamentations-terms.md); what is left
is listed in the two pull requests' open questions. Claims about the Greek
text, ketiv/qere and other ancient sources were written from general knowledge
and are unverified until a human checks them.

These are notes for reviewers, not part of the translation.
