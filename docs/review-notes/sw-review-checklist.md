# Swahili review checklist

Where a native Swahili reviewer starts. All 66 books are drafted in Swahili
(about 30,600 records), and every record is `ai_draft`. The drafts follow
`docs/swahili-style.md` and the terms docs listed below. Each of those docs is
a set of proposals waiting for a reviewer.

How to use it:

- **Settle section 1 first.** Each item there is a choice made once for a
  whole book or for the whole Bible, so one decision fixes wording in hundreds
  or thousands of verses. Items are grouped by kind and, within each group,
  put in order of how much text they touch. The counts come from the Swahili
  in `content/` (all three levels together) and are approximate.
- Then work through section 2 (choices the drafters marked uncertain again and
  again), section 3 (places where the docs or the drafts disagree) and
  section 4 (passages to check against the copyrighted Swahili versions).
- The full list of uncertain Swahili decisions, with links, is in
  `docs/review-notes/sw-uncertain.md`: 502 decisions in 56 books. It is
  generated by `python3 scripts/review_checklist.py sw --write`, so
  regenerate it after the content changes rather than editing it.
- **To change a settled choice**, edit the terms doc that records it (named in
  each item), then revise every passage drafted under it, at every level, and
  the rendering decision at its first use in each book. A choice that changes
  in a doc but not in `content/` becomes one more item for section 3.
- Records stay `ai_draft` until a named human reviewer approves them. Ticking
  a box here records a decision; it does not change any record's status.

Levels are L (Halisi, Literal), B (Linganifu, Balanced) and R (Rahisi,
Readable). "Every level" means L, B and R read the same.

Terms docs with Swahili sections (cited below by short name):

- **Style**: `docs/swahili-style.md` (John and the whole language)
- **NT**: `docs/nt-spanish-swahili-terms.md` (rest of the New Testament)
- **Pentateuch**: `docs/pentateuch-fr-es-sw-terms.md`
- **Joshua–Ruth**: `docs/joshua-ruth-five-languages-terms.md`
- **Samuel**: `docs/samuel-five-languages-terms.md`
- **Kings–Chronicles**: `docs/kings-chronicles-five-languages-terms.md`
- **Psalms–Proverbs**: `docs/psalms-proverbs-five-languages-terms.md`
- **Isaiah–Ezra**: `docs/isaiah-jeremiah-ezra-five-languages-terms.md`, section "Swahili"
- **Nehemiah–Song**: `docs/nehemiah-to-song-five-languages-terms.md`, section "Swahili"
- **Prophets**: `docs/lamentations-ezekiel-daniel-twelve-five-languages-terms.md`, section "Swahili"

## 1. Settle these first

### Names of God and setting spans

- [ ] **The divine name: `{{divine_name:BWANA|Yahwe}}`.** Default *BWANA* in
  capitals, the reader setting shows *Yahwe*. Same at every level, with
  *BWANA Mungu* for "the LORD God" and *malaika wa BWANA* for the angel of the
  LORD. Alternatives: only the setting's *Yahwe*; no other default was
  considered. About 19,700 spans in 5,600 records, every Old Testament book
  except Esther and Ecclesiastes. Pentateuch, "The names of God". Exodus 3:14–15
  and 6:2–3 follow the English: [EXO 3:14](https://openwordbible.org/sw/exo/3/#v14), [EXO 6:3](https://openwordbible.org/sw/exo/6/#v3) (both marked
  uncertain, including *NIKO NILIYE* and *NIKO* as a name).
- [ ] **`deity_pronoun` only on free pronouns and possessives**
  (`{{deity_pronoun:yeye|Yeye}}`, `wake|Wake`, `lake|Lake`, `nami|Nami`,
  `kwake|Kwake` ...), never on a verb prefix or object marker, which cannot
  take a capital. So Swahili has fewer spans than English, and a pronoun that
  opens a sentence is written plainly with its capital. Alternative: drop the
  setting in Swahili. About 22,300 spans in 6,100 records. Style §7;
  Nehemiah–Song "Settings recap"; Prophets §4. Check that the possessive in
  each span agrees with its noun (*utukufu wake*, *jina lake*, *habari zake*).
- [ ] **"Lord GOD" (*Adonai YHWH*): `Bwana {{divine_name:MUNGU|Yahwe}}`**,
  span on MUNGU only; with hosts `Bwana MUNGU wa majeshi`. About 930 spans in
  300 records, two thirds of them in Ezekiel. Pentateuch, "The names of God";
  Prophets §1.
- [ ] **`christos`: `{{christos:Kristo|Masihi}}`**, `Yesu {{christos:Kristo|Masihi}}`;
  plural `{{christos:makristo|masihi}} wa uongo`. Swahili has no article, so
  both read naturally at every level. About 1,640 spans in 480 records. Style §7;
  NT, "Recurring terms". See section 3 for John 1:41 and 4:25.
- [ ] **`units`: metric by default, the original unit as the alternative**
  (`{{units:karibu lita 22|efa moja}}`); Literal keeps the original unit and
  has no span. This differs from the English default (US units) and is
  recorded as a rendering decision. Decimals with a point, thousands with a
  comma (*dhiraa 2,000*). Ezekiel 40–48 uses the long cubit (53 cm). About
  1,270 spans in 400 records. Style §7; Pentateuch, "All three languages";
  Prophets §1 (unit table).
- [ ] **`gender`: normally no span**, because *mtu*, *watu*, *wanadamu*,
  *ndugu*, *watoto* and *-ake* already include women, and the only
  "traditional" alternative (*wanaume*) would change the meaning. Only 13 spans
  exist (for example *wahenga|mababu*, John 6:49, 58). Alternative: mirror the
  English spans. Affects every place the English has a gender span (thousands,
  about 137 in Ecclesiastes alone). Style §7; Prophets §4.
- [ ] **The short name *Yah*: `{{divine_name:BWANA|Yah}}`** (*Msifuni BWANA*,
  "Praise the LORD"). 135 spans in 43 records, mostly Psalms; Song 8:6 at L
  only ([SNG 8:6](https://openwordbible.org/sw/sng/8/#v6), uncertain). Psalms–Proverbs, "Psalm titles".

### Address forms and speech levels

- [ ] **Singular and plural "you" without a setting.** *wewe*/*ninyi* and the
  prefixes *u-*/*m-* carry the Greek and Hebrew distinction; God and Jesus are
  addressed as *wewe*. There is no T–V distinction, so respect is carried by
  words: *bwana wangu mfalme*, *Ee mfalme*, *mtumishi wako*. Courtiers:
  *Ee mfalme, uishi milele!* (L, B), *Mtukufu mfalme, uishi milele!* (R).
  Affects every speech. Style §6; Nehemiah–Song "Settings recap"; Prophets §3.
- [ ] **Lord and lord: *Bwana* / *bwana*.** One word for *kyrios* and *Adonai*:
  capital *Bwana* for God and for Jesus as believers address him, lowercase
  *bwana* as polite address ("Sir") and for masters in parables; *Bwana Mkuu*
  for *despotēs*. About 3,500 *Bwana* and 950 *bwana*. Style §5; NT table.
  Where a speaker's faith is unclear the capital is a judgment call
  ([ACT 9:5](https://openwordbible.org/sw/act/9/#v5), [2TI 2:21](https://openwordbible.org/sw/2ti/2/#v21), both uncertain).
- [ ] **Vocative *Ee*:** *Ee BWANA*, *Ee Mungu wangu*, *Ee Bwana MUNGU*, as the
  published Psalms. Psalms, prayers and the prophets throughout. Isaiah–Ezra,
  "Formulas"; Prophets §3.
- [ ] **"Woman" (*gynai*) as *Mama* at every level**, because *Mwanamke!* said
  to a woman's face is rude in Swahili. 16 records ([JHN 2:4](https://openwordbible.org/sw/jhn/2/#v4), [JHN 19:26](https://openwordbible.org/sw/jhn/19/#v26),
  both uncertain). Style §5.

### Key theological terms

- [ ] ***ḥesed*: *upendo usiokoma*** for God's steadfast love (every level in
  the Psalms, the prophets and Lamentations; L and B in Exodus), *fadhili* for kindness between
  people. Alternatives considered: *fadhili*, *rehema* ("leave out the loyalty
  of a love that does not give up", [EXO 15:13](https://openwordbible.org/sw/exo/15/#v13)); Exodus 34:6 R says *wema*. About 580 *usiokoma* in 220
  records and 140 *fadhili* in 70. Pentateuch table; Psalms–Proverbs table;
  Samuel table (which gives *fadhili* at B, R); Prophets §1. **Samuel to
  Chronicles disagree with the rest: see section 3.**
- [ ] ***ʿeved* / *doulos*: *mtumwa* (L), *mtumishi* (B, R)**, with *mtumwa* at
  every level where slavery itself is the point (Egypt, slaves bought and sold,
  slavery to sin), and *mtumishi* at every level for God's named servants
  (Moses, David, "your servant" in prayer, the servant of Isaiah). Female
  slaves get no separate word (*mjakazi* only where marked). About 780
  *mtumwa* and 2,000 *mtumishi*. Style §5; NT table; Pentateuch table;
  Joshua–Ruth and Psalms–Proverbs "Settled while drafting"; Isaiah–Ezra
  "Isaiah". 13 uncertain decisions (section 2).
- [ ] ***haki* for righteousness, and never for "a right".** *tsedeq*,
  *dikaiosynē*: *haki*; "the righteous" *mwenye haki*. "Right, authority" is
  *mamlaka*, *uwezo* or *ruhusa*, a birthright *stahili* ([GEN 25:31](https://openwordbible.org/sw/gen/25/#v31)). Court
  justice may share *haki*; *mishpat* is *hukumu* (L) / *haki* (B, R) in Isaiah,
  *hukumu* / *haki* (L) and *haki* / *uadilifu* (B, R) in Amos 5. About 2,400
  occurrences in 900 records. Style §5; NT table; Isaiah–Ezra "Isaiah";
  Prophets §1. [2TH 3:9](https://openwordbible.org/sw/2th/3/#v9) uses *haki* for *exousia* (uncertain): settle it.
- [ ] **God's law and rules.** *torah* of God: *sheria* (L), *mafundisho* (B, R),
  but *mafundisho* at every level in Psalm 119 and *sheria* at L and B in
  Jeremiah 31:33 (for Hebrews 8:10). Statute *kanuni*, rule *agizo*,
  commandment *amri* (Pentateuch); Ezekiel uses *amri* / *hukumu* (L),
  *amri* / *maagizo* (B), *sheria* (R) for statutes and judgments. About 1,470
  *sheria*, 420 *mafundisho*, 400 *kanuni*, 1,100 *amri*. Pentateuch table;
  Psalms–Proverbs "Settled while drafting" (flags it for a reviewer);
  Isaiah–Ezra "Jeremiah"; Prophets §1 "Ezekiel". **Applied unevenly: see
  section 3.**
- [ ] ***kweli* for truth**, the older noun, also "true" (*nuru ya kweli*), so
  John's link survives. Alternative: *ukweli*, the everyday modern noun, perhaps
  at R. About 1,850 occurrences in 1,000 records. Style §5 ([JHN 1:14](https://openwordbible.org/sw/jhn/1/#v14),
  uncertain).
- [ ] ***imani* / *kuamini*** for faith and believe, the verb kept at every
  level; Habakkuk 2:4 uses *uaminifu* for *ʾemunah*. About 1,150 occurrences in
  510 records. Style §5; NT table; Prophets §1 "The Twelve".
- [ ] ***utukufu*, *neema*, *amani*** for glory, grace and peace, the same word
  at every level. About 950, 420 and many hundreds of occurrences. Style §5.
- [ ] **Son of Man: *Mwana wa Adamu*** (capitals, no gender span). Alternative:
  *Mwana wa Mtu*, more literal. Daniel 7:13 and Revelation 1:13 say *mwana wa
  mtu* (lowercase). 248 occurrences in 80 records, all four Gospels, Acts,
  Revelation. Style §5; NT table; Prophets §1 "Daniel". 7 uncertain decisions.
- [ ] **Ezekiel's *ben adam*: *mwana wa binadamu*** (lowercase, every level,
  kept apart from the Gospel title). Alternative: *mwanadamu*, the ordinary word
  for a human being, at R. About 290 occurrences in 98 records (Ezekiel and
  Daniel 8:17). Prophets §1 "Ezekiel" (uncertain).
- [ ] **The Word: *Neno* with personal agreement** (*Neno alikuwa*, *Neno akawa
  mwili*), although *neno* is class 5. The same question runs through every
  personified noun: *Roho* (*Roho aliye juu yako*), Wisdom in Proverbs 1 and 8,
  *pepo*, *joka*, *Mtetezi*. [JHN 1:1](https://openwordbible.org/sw/jhn/1/#v1) and 26 more uncertain decisions
  (section 2). Style §5.
- [ ] ***uzima* for life (*zōē*), *uzima wa milele* for eternal life**; *maisha*
  is a lifetime, *nafsi* the soul where set beside the body. About 550
  *uzima* in 210 records. Style §5; NT table ("life (*psychē*)").
- [ ] **Sheol and Hades: *Kuzimu* at every level.** Readable may say *kaburi*
  where the English Readable says "the grave" (Hosea 13:14). Gehenna is
  *Gehena* (L), *jehanamu* (B, R). About 215 *Kuzimu* in 75 records.
  Pentateuch table; Psalms–Proverbs "Settled while drafting"; NT table.
- [ ] **Spirits: *Roho* (capital) for God's Spirit, *roho* for a human spirit,
  *pepo* for demons and unclean spirits** (*pepo mchafu* L, B; *pepo mbaya* R),
  *upepo* for wind (never plural *pepo*), *pumzi* for breath. About 385 *pepo*.
  NT table; Prophets §1.
- [ ] **"The Jews": *Wayahudi* (L); *viongozi wa Wayahudi* (B, R)** where
  John means the authorities. About 850 *Wayahudi*, 58 *viongozi wa Wayahudi*.
  Style §5, following `docs/john-terms.md`.
- [ ] **Sign: *ishara*, never *muujiza*** (miracle); "mighty works" *matendo ya
  nguvu* (L, B), *miujiza* (R). About 530 occurrences in 200 records. Style §5;
  NT table.
- [ ] **The skin disease (*tsaraʿat*, *lepra*): *ugonjwa mbaya wa ngozi***,
  never *ukoma*, in the Old Testament. The NT terms doc says nothing, and the
  Gospels use *ukoma*. About 150 occurrences in 59 records, chiefly Leviticus
  13–14. Pentateuch table; Kings–Chronicles "Settled while drafting". **See
  section 3.**
- [ ] **The Most High: *Aliye Juu Kabisa*** (Genesis 14, Psalms, Daniel, Luke,
  Hebrews 7:1); R may add *Mungu*. About 135 occurrences in 53 records.
  NT table; Prophets §1. **Three forms in use: see section 3.**
- [ ] ***hassatan*: *yule satani* (L), *Mshtaki* (B, R)** in Job 1–2 and
  Zechariah 3, kept apart from the name *Shetani* (1 Chronicles 21:1 and the
  New Testament). Nehemiah–Song "Job" and "Before review"; Prophets §1 "The
  Twelve". **Job and Zechariah spell the Literal differently: see section 3.**
- [ ] **The anointed: *masihi wa BWANA* (L); *mfalme wa BWANA aliyempaka mafuta*
  (B); *mfalme ambaye BWANA amempaka mafuta* (R)**; never *Masihi* for Daniel
  9:25. Samuel table; Psalms–Proverbs table; Prophets §1 "Daniel".

### Recurring formulas

- [ ] **"Thus says the LORD":** *BWANA asema hivi:* (L); *Hivi ndivyo BWANA
  asemavyo:* (B); *Hili ndilo BWANA analosema:* (R); the same with *Bwana
  MUNGU*. About 1,400 occurrences in 530 records. Kings–Chronicles "Settled
  while drafting"; Isaiah–Ezra "Formulas"; Prophets §1. **Exodus to Samuel
  have L and B the other way round: see section 3.**
- [ ] **"Declares the LORD" (*neʾum YHWH*):** *asema BWANA* (L, B); *anasema
  BWANA* (R); also *asema Bwana MUNGU*. About 1,250 occurrences in 400
  records, half of them in Jeremiah. Isaiah–Ezra "Formulas"; Prophets §1.
- [ ] **"The LORD of hosts": *BWANA wa majeshi* at every level**, never
  *Sabaoth*; *BWANA Mungu wa majeshi*. About 240 places, mostly Jeremiah,
  Isaiah, Zechariah, Malachi, Haggai. Samuel table; Psalms–Proverbs "Settled
  while drafting"; Isaiah–Ezra "Formulas".
- [ ] **"The word of the LORD came to me":** *Neno la BWANA likanijia, kusema:*
  (L); *Neno la BWANA likanijia:* (B); *BWANA alinipa ujumbe huu:* (R); book
  headings to match. About 240 occurrences in 130 records. Isaiah–Ezra
  "Formulas"; Prophets §1; Kings–Chronicles regnal formulas.
- [ ] **"Woe" (*hoy*, *ouai*):** *Ole, ...* (L); *Ole wao ...* (B); *Itakuwa
  vibaya sana kwa ...* (R); of oneself *Ole wangu* / *Mambo ni mabaya sana
  kwangu*; funeral cry *Ole!* / *Inasikitisha!* About 275 occurrences in 126
  records. Isaiah–Ezra "Formulas"; NT table.
- [ ] **"Truly I tell you":** *Kweli nawaambia* (L, B), *Nawaambia ukweli* (R);
  John's double *amēn* *Kweli kweli nawaambia* (L, B); to one person *Kweli
  nakuambia* / *Nakuambia ukweli*. About 80 sayings in the Gospels. Style §5;
  NT table.
- [ ] **Ezekiel's "then you will know that I am the LORD":** *nanyi mtajua
  kwamba mimi [ni] BWANA* (L); *Ndipo mtajua kwamba mimi ndimi BWANA* (B);
  *... mimi ni BWANA* (R). About 65 records. Prophets §1 "Ezekiel".
- [ ] **The regnal formulas of Kings and Chronicles** (accession, mother,
  verdict *alifanya maovu / yaliyo sawa machoni pa BWANA*, the sources, death
  *alilala pamoja na baba zake* (L) / *alipumzika pamoja na babu zake* (B) /
  *alikufa* (R), successor, high places, "to this day"). One wording per
  level through four books; the verdict alone occurs 61 times. Kings–Chronicles
  "The regnal formulas".
- [ ] **"In that day":** *katika siku ile* (L); *siku hiyo* (B); *wakati huo*
  (R). About 65 places, mostly Isaiah and Zechariah. Isaiah–Ezra
  "Formulas"; Prophets §1.
- [ ] ***Selah*: *Sela*** at the end of its verse at every level. 81 records
  (Psalms, Habakkuk 3). Psalm titles: *Kwa kiongozi wa kwaya* (L, B), *Kwa
  kiongozi wa waimbaji* (R); *zaburi*, *wimbo*; *maskili*, *mikitamu*,
  *shigayoni* kept as names at L and B. Psalms–Proverbs "Psalm titles"; 29
  uncertain decisions (section 2).

### Names and spelling conventions

- [ ] **Published forms win; new names follow one pattern**: a final vowel
  after a closed syllable (*-i* after most consonants, *-u* after *m* and *b*),
  *y* for Hebrew *j*, *th* and *sh* kept (*Yeremia*, *Yoshua*, *Kalebu*,
  *Moabu*). Each verse keeps its own Hebrew form (*Nebukadneza* /
  *Nebukadreza*, *Yekonia* / *Konia*, *Hiramu* / *Huramu*). Every narrative
  book; about 35 new-name decisions are marked uncertain (section 2).
  Pentateuch, "Names"; Prophets §2; Kings–Chronicles "Settled while drafting".
- [ ] **Familiar Swahili forms of the main names**: *Yesu*, *Musa*, *Daudi*,
  *Sulemani*, *Ibrahimu* (NT) / *Abrahamu* (OT and John), *Yusufu* (Joseph and
  Mary's husband) / *Yosefu* (of Arimathea), *Uyahudi* for Judea (never
  *Yudea*), *Yerusalemu*, *Sayuni*. Style §4; NT "Swahili"; Pentateuch
  "Names". *Abrahamu* / *Ibrahimu* and *Sulemani* / *Solomoni* are not yet
  even across the Testaments (section 3).
- [ ] **Ecclesiastes: *Kohelethi* (L), *Mwalimu* (B, R)** beside a book title
  that says *Mhubiri*; *hevel* is *pumzi* (L), *pumzi tu* (B), *kama pumzi* /
  *haidumu* (R), never *ubatili*. Every chapter of the book (*pumzi* about 100
  times). Nehemiah–Song "Ecclesiastes" and "Before review".
- [ ] **Susa is *Shushani*** (Ezra 4:9, Esther, Daniel 8:2), though modern
  usage may prefer *Susa*. Nehemiah–Song "Before review".
- [ ] **Swahili time:** "the ninth hour" is *saa tisa* at every level, because
  Swahili also counts from sunrise; B and R may add *mchana*, *jioni*. Gospels
  and Acts. Style §4; NT "Swahili".

### Style

- [ ] **Narrative tense: *-li-* for the first verb, then *-ka-*** (B, R); L keeps
  the Greek historic present (*anasema*). Laws: subjunctive or *-ta-* (L, B),
  *lazima* or plain commands (R). Oracles: *-ta-*, *-na-*/*-a-* or *-me-*, not
  the narrative chain. Every narrative book. Style §3; Pentateuch "All three
  languages"; Prophets §4.
- [ ] **Quotation marks** as in English, stored straight: “ ” outside, ‘ ’
  inside. Oracles, God's first-person speech, Job, Qoheleth and the Song take
  none; speech quoted inside them does. Every book. Style §4; Isaiah–Ezra
  "All five languages"; Nehemiah–Song "All five languages".
- [ ] **Numbers: words or figures?** The style guide says words in running
  text and figures for measures; the Nehemiah doc says figures above 99 and for
  list totals; the Prophets doc says words, with figures from 100 for
  measures. The drafts mix them (section 3). About 400 records have *mia*.
  Style §4; Samuel and Kings–Chronicles "Before review"; Nehemiah–Song "All
  five languages"; Prophets §4.
- [ ] **Literal brackets**: words Swahili needs that the source lacks go in
  [square brackets]; subject prefixes and object markers are never bracketed,
  and there is no counterpart to the English "[the]". All Literal text. Style
  §3; Prophets §4.
- [ ] **Lines follow the English record** (`\q` only where the English level
  has them), with breaks at Swahili phrase boundaries, never between a verb and
  its object marker or a noun and its adjective. Psalms, Proverbs, Job, the
  Song, the prophets. Psalms–Proverbs, Nehemiah–Song and Prophets, "All five
  languages"; Prophets §4.
- [ ] **Spelling**: Kiswahili Sanifu as written in Tanzania and Kenya, no Sheng;
  *ng'* with the apostrophe. Capitals for *Mungu*, *Baba*, *Mwana*, *Roho
  Mtakatifu*, *Maandiko*; *sheria* lowercase. Style §4.

## 2. Recurring uncertain choices

Clusters found by grouping `docs/review-notes/sw-uncertain.md` (502 items).
Counts are items in the generated list; one item often covers a whole psalm,
chapter or book. Settling a cluster settles every item in it.

- [ ] **Plants, animals, gems, spices and materials with no everyday Swahili
  name** (about 45): the drafters borrowed (*pistachio*, *spelti*, *kasia*),
  transcribed the Hebrew (*natafu*, *shehelethi*, *helbena*) or used a near
  name (*mkwaju* for tamarisk, which is the tamarind). [GEN 21:33](https://openwordbible.org/sw/gen/21/#v33),
  [EXO 30:34](https://openwordbible.org/sw/exo/30/#v34), [LEV 11:30](https://openwordbible.org/sw/lev/11/#v30).
- [ ] **Psalm titles and musical terms** (29): which Hebrew terms stay as names
  at L and B (*gitithi*, *shoshanimu*, *mahalathi*, *maskili*), how R explains
  them, and *Sela*. [PSA 8:1](https://openwordbible.org/sw/psa/8/#v1), [PSA 32:1](https://openwordbible.org/sw/psa/32/#v1), [PSA 45:1](https://openwordbible.org/sw/psa/45/#v1).
- [ ] **Personal or noun-class agreement** (27): personified or spiritual nouns
  given personal agreement (*Neno*, *Roho*, Wisdom, *pepo*, *joka*, *Haki* as a
  goddess), and nouns that need a class decision (*kanisa* class 5, *Babeli*
  class 3, Israel singular or plural, a singular verb after "God and our
  Lord"). [JHN 1:1](https://openwordbible.org/sw/jhn/1/#v1), [PRO 1:20](https://openwordbible.org/sw/pro/1/#v20), [REV 12:3](https://openwordbible.org/sw/rev/12/#v3).
- [ ] **Names: new transcriptions and clashes** (about 35): forms with an
  unwanted Swahili meaning (*Shiba* "be full", [GEN 26:33](https://openwordbible.org/sw/gen/26/#v33)), two Hebrew
  names that become one (*Eri*, [GEN 46:16](https://openwordbible.org/sw/gen/46/#v16)), new Egyptian and border names
  in Ezekiel ([EZK 30:15](https://openwordbible.org/sw/ezk/30/#v15), [EZK 47:16](https://openwordbible.org/sw/ezk/47/#v16)).
- [ ] **Tabernacle and temple building words** (about 21): *fremu*, *ndimi* /
  *vigingi*, *ukingo*, *vikalio*, *birika*, *kijumba*, *miimo*, *eneo
  lililotengwa*, *roshani*. [EXO 26:15](https://openwordbible.org/sw/exo/26/#v15), [EXO 36:22](https://openwordbible.org/sw/exo/36/#v22), [EZK 40:7](https://openwordbible.org/sw/ezk/40/#v7).
- [ ] **"Son of man"** (14): *mwana wa binadamu* in Ezekiel and Daniel 8:17,
  *Mwana wa Adamu* in the Gospels, *mwana wa mtu* in Revelation 1:13.

doc, then align the drafts.
