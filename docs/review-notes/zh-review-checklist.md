# Chinese review checklist

The starting point for native Chinese reviewers (Simplified characters,
Mainland usage). **This is an outline written before drafting began, and
before Chinese is registered as a reading language**: it lists the
book-wide choices the drafts will follow, in the order a reviewer should
settle them, so that the most consequential ones can be settled before the
first book is drafted, and review of the text can start as soon as it is.
The counts, the recurring uncertain decisions, the inconsistencies and the
passage list are filled in from `content/` once books are drafted (the
bracketed notes say how).

Most of the Chinese wording will follow a few dozen book-wide choices: the
word for God, the divine name, the pronouns for God, the forms of address,
the key terms, the recurring formulas, the name forms. A reviewer who settles
those first settles the wording of thousands of verses at once, so start
with section 1. Several of them (the `god_term` setting, the `deity_pronoun`
pairs, the quotation marks) also decide what code the registration pull
request has to change, so they are best settled before it.

How to use it:

- **Work down section 1 first.** Each item decides wording in many verses.
  Tick it when you agree, or write the change you want beside it.
- **Then section 2**, the uncertain decisions that recur: each cluster is
  one question asked many times; answer it once.
- **Then sections 3 and 4**: places where the drafts disagree with each
  other, and the famous passages to check against the Chinese Union Version
  and the modern versions for originality.
- The full list of Chinese decisions marked uncertain, with links, will be
  `docs/review-notes/zh-uncertain.md`, generated by
  `python3 scripts/review_checklist.py zh --write` (do not edit it by hand).
- **Changing a choice** means editing the doc that records it (the style
  guide or the glossary; each item names the section) and then revising
  every passage drafted under it, at each level it applies to, with the
  rendering decisions that cite it. A choice is not changed by editing one
  verse.
- Records stay `ai_draft` until a named human reviewer approves them.
  Nothing here is marked reviewed by Claude.

Doc names below are short: "Style" is `docs/chinese-style.md`, "Terms" is
`docs/chinese-terms.md`. Levels are L (直译), B (均衡) and R (易读). [Counts:
Balanced renderings unless stated, from `content/` on the date the checklist
is filled in.]

## 1. Settle these first

### God, the divine name and setting spans

- [ ] **God is a Chinese-only reader setting, `{{god_term:神|上帝}}`**, default
  神, for every word naming the one God (*theos*; *Elohim* of the God of
  Israel), at every level; gods and idols 神 / 神明 with no span.
  Alternatives: 上帝 as the default; no setting, a fixed 神. Style §4.
  [count]
- [ ] **YHWH is `{{divine_name:上主|雅威}}`** at every level: the title 上主
  by default, as "the LORD"; the name 雅威 as the setting. 耶和华 is not used.
  Alternatives: 耶和华 as the setting's name; 主 as the default (merging YHWH
  with *Adonai*). Style §4; Terms "Names of God". [count, books]
- [ ] ***ʾAdonai* YHWH is `主宰{{divine_name:上主|雅威}}`** (主宰上主), to avoid
  主上主 being read as 主上 + 主. Alternative: `主{{divine_name:上主|雅威}}`.
  Style §4. [count; about 200 in Ezekiel]
- [ ] **YHWH *Elohim* is `{{divine_name:上主|雅威}}{{god_term:神|上帝}}`**
  (上主神); YHWH *tsevaot* 万军之上主 (L, B), 统领万军的上主 (R); *Yah*
  `{{divine_name:上主|雅}}`. Alternative: 雅威 for *Yah* too. Style §4; Terms.
- [ ] **Lord is 主; a human master 主人; "sir" 先生.** Style §4.
- [ ] **Pronouns for God under `deity_pronoun`**: `{{deity_pronoun:他|祂}}`,
  `{{deity_pronoun:你|祢}}`, reverent form shown by default, as the English
  capital is (needs a validator change). Alternative: no spans, 他 and 你
  plainly, as the CUV and Mainland general usage. Style §4. [count]
- [ ] **Who takes a pronoun span**: whoever the English spans (God, Christ;
  the angel of the LORD speaking as God; the king of Psalms 45 and 110 only
  if the English does); not angels as messengers, not figures in parables.
  Style §4. [uncertain decisions that turn on it]
- [ ] **Christ under `christos`**: `{{christos:基督|弥赛亚}}`; with the name
  `耶稣{{christos:基督|弥赛亚}}`, Paul's order `{{christos:基督|弥赛亚}}耶稣`;
  弥赛亚 plainly at John 1:41 and 4:25. Style §8. [count]
- [ ] **Gender spans**: `{{gender:弟兄姐妹|弟兄}}` for believers;
  `{{gender:儿女|儿子}}`, `{{gender:人|男人}}`, `{{gender:祖先|列祖}}` where
  Chinese has two wordings; most English pronoun spans become 人 / 自己 with
  no span. Alternative: 弟兄 with no span, as the Chinese Bibles. Style §5,
  §8. [count]
- [ ] **Units under `units`**: metric by default at B and R in Arabic digits
  (约45厘米), the original unit as the alternative in Chinese numerals,
  Literal the original unit only; coins as wages. Terms "Land, life and
  measures". [count]

### Address forms and speech

- [ ] **God and Jesus are addressed as 你** (with the span) at every level,
  by everyone. Alternative: 您 at B and R. Style §5. [count]
- [ ] **Literal uses 你 for every singular "you"**; B and R use 您 for
  respectful address to a king, master, governor or official. Style §5.
  [count of 您; uncertain register decisions]
- [ ] **Kings**: 王啊 (L), 陛下 with 您 (B, R); "my lord the king" 我主我王
  (L, B), 陛下 (R). Kings say 我, never 朕. Style §5; Terms "Address".
- [ ] **"Your servant" for "I"** kept as 仆人 at L (and B where the English
  B keeps it); 我 at R. Style §5.
- [ ] **"Woman" in address**: 妇人 (L), 女士 (B, R), Jesus's mother
  included. Alternative: 母亲 at R for his mother. Style §5.
- [ ] **We is 我们** everywhere; 咱们 is not used. Plural "you" 你们; 您们 is
  not used. Style §5.
- [ ] **Verbs of speaking**: 说 for everyone, God and Jesus included. Style §5.

### Key theological terms

- [ ] **Key terms**: 恩典 (grace), 信心 / 信 (faith, believe), 义 (righteousness),
  称义 (justify), 真理 (truth), 荣耀 (glory), 救恩 (salvation), 福音 / 好消息 (R),
  平安 / 和平 (peace), 圣灵 (Spirit), 约 / 盟约 (R) (covenant), 教会 (church),
  悔改 (repent), 罪 (sin), 圣 / 圣洁 (holy), 永生 (eternal life), 道 (the Word,
  John 1). Style §7.
- [ ] **Grace is 恩典**, not 恩惠 or 恩宠. Style §7.
- [ ] ***hesed* is 慈爱** (L, B), 始终不变的爱 (R) for God's; 恩情 / 善待 between
  people; 忠诚的爱 / 忠心 for Israel's loyalty (Hosea). Alternative: 坚定的爱.
  Terms "Hesed".
- [ ] **Sheol is 阴间** (L, B), 死亡之地 (R); the Pit 坑 / 深坑 / 坟墓. Terms.
- [ ] **Torah**: 律法 (the Law, Psalms L, B); 教导 in the Prophets and
  Proverbs. Terms "Torah, law".
- [ ] **Sacrifices**: 燔祭, 素祭, 赎罪祭, 赎过祭, 平安祭 (uncertain: 和好祭,
  团契祭); 赎罪盖 (alternative 施恩座). Terms "Worship and the sanctuary".
- [ ] **Sanctuary**: 会幕 (tent of meeting), 帐幕 (tabernacle), 约柜 (ark),
  圣殿 / 殿 (temple), 至圣所, 圣所. Terms.
- [ ] ***doulos* / *ʿeved***: 奴隶 (L), 仆人 (B, R); 奴隶 where slavery is the
  point; 仆人 for Moses, David and the psalmist at every level. Style §7;
  Terms.
- [ ] **Advocate (*paraklētos*) is 辩护者** (L, B), 帮助者 (R); alternative
  保惠师. Style §7.
- [ ] **Demons and the devil**: 鬼 (L) / 邪灵 (B, R); 魔鬼; 撒但 (alternative
  撒旦). Style §7.
- [ ] **Gentiles**: 外邦人 (L, B), 非犹太人 (R). Style §7.

### Recurring formulas

- [ ] 上主如此说 (L), 上主这样说 (B), 上主说 (R) for *koh ʾamar YHWH*; 这是上主的
  宣告 (L), 这是上主的话 (B), 上主说 (R) for *neʾum YHWH*; 上主的话临到…… (L, B).
  Style §12; Terms "The Prophets". [count]
- [ ] Truly I tell you: 阿们，我告诉你们 (L), 我确实告诉你们 (B), 我把实话告诉你们 (R).
  Style §7.
- [ ] *hinneh* / *idou*: 看哪 (L), 看 or left out (B), left out (R). Style §12.
- [ ] Ezekiel's "son of man": 人子啊 (L), 人哪 (B, R). Alternative: 人子 at
  every level. Style §12.
- [ ] The regnal formulas of Kings and Chronicles. Terms.
- [ ] Psalm titles: 交给指挥; 大卫的诗; 上行之诗; 细拉. Terms "Psalm titles".

### Names and spelling

- [ ] **The Protestant forms in common use** (耶稣, 摩西, 大卫, 所罗门, 约翰,
  彼得, 保罗, 雅各 for both Jacob and James), not the Catholic forms (梅瑟,
  达味, 若望, 伯多禄, 保禄). Style §6; Terms "Names".
- [ ] **Modern standard forms for places that still exist** (大马士革, 塞浦路斯,
  克里特, 马耳他), and 凯撒 / 凯撒利亚 for Caesar and Caesarea. Alternative: the
  CUV's forms (大马色, 该撒). Style §6.
- [ ] **Persian kings** in their Bible forms (古列, 大利乌, 亚达薛西, 亚哈随鲁).
  Alternative: 居鲁士, 大流士, 阿尔塔薛西斯, 薛西斯. Terms.
- [ ] **Philip the apostle 腓力, Philip the evangelist 腓利**, kept apart as in
  the Chinese Bibles. Terms.
- [ ] **Forms coined while drafting** (Terms "Added while drafting"). [list
  each with its first verse]
- [ ] **Book names** as the Protestant Bibles (创世记 ... 启示录). Style §6.

### Punctuation, numbers and typography

- [ ] **Quotation marks “ ” outside, ‘ ’ inside**, written curly in the data;
  「 」 not used. Style §9. (Needs the quotation checker change, Style §14.)
- [ ] **Full-width punctuation** ，、；：？！。, …… and ——, 《 》 for book titles;
  no spaces between characters. Style §9.
- [ ] **Speech**: colon before it; the stop inside the closing mark when the
  quotation is a whole sentence. Style §9.
- [ ] **Numbers**: Chinese numerals in the text (十二个门徒, 四万六千五百);
  Arabic digits only in modern measures (约3公里). Style §10.
- [ ] **Simplified characters** of the 通用规范汉字表, Mainland word forms (着,
  里, 于), 的 / 地 / 得 kept apart. Style §3.

### Style

- [ ] **Register**: no 文言 grammar and none of the CUV's period forms
  (甚么, 晓得, 归与, 夫子), no colloquial or regional words (咱们, 啥, 俺).
  Style §3. [search counts]
- [ ] **Literal brackets**: measure words, 了 / 着 / 过, 是, 们 are not
  bracketed. Style §3.
- [ ] **Readable**: common characters, short sentences, no hard 成语. Style §3.
- [ ] **Level names**: 直译, 均衡, 易读. Alternatives: 逐字 / 标准 / 浅白.
  Style §3.
