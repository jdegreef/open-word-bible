# Vietnamese review checklist

## 1. What this is for

Where a native Vietnamese reviewer starts. Every book of the Bible is drafted
in Vietnamese and every record is `ai_draft`. This page puts the choices that
decide the most wording first, so that one decision settles thousands of
verses before anyone reads verse by verse.

How to use it:

- **Settle section 2 first.** Each item there is a book-wide choice: it
  decides wording in many verses, often in many books. Tick it once you have
  confirmed it or decided what replaces it.
- Then work through section 3, the recurring uncertain decisions, and
  section 4, the places where the drafts disagree with each other. Section 5
  lists the passages to read against the well-known Vietnamese versions.
- The full list of the 964 Vietnamese rendering decisions marked uncertain,
  with links, is `docs/review-notes/vi-uncertain.md` (generated by
  `scripts/review_checklist.py`; do not edit it by hand).
- **Changing a settled choice** means editing the terms doc that records it
  (the doc and section are given with each item) and then revising every
  passage drafted under it, at each level it applies to, with the rendering
  decisions that cite it. A choice is not changed by editing one verse.
- Records stay `ai_draft` until a named human reviewer approves them. Nothing
  here is marked reviewed by Claude.
- Three things are **settled by the founder** and are raised with the founder
  rather than changed here (`docs/vietnamese-style.md`, "Settled"): the
  Protestant tradition for names and key terms (*Đức Chúa Trời*, *Giăng*,
  *Phi-e-rơ*, *Đức Giê-xu*, *làm báp-têm*, *Y-sơ-ra-ên*), and *nói* for Jesus
  speaking with *phán* kept for God.

Levels are written L (Sát nghĩa), B (Cân bằng) and R (Dễ đọc). "occ" counts
occurrences in the Vietnamese text of all three levels; "records" counts
sentences. Counts were taken from `content/` on 30 October 2026.

- **Joshua–Ruth**: `docs/joshua-ruth-five-languages-terms.md`
- **Samuel**: `docs/samuel-five-languages-terms.md`
- **Kings**: `docs/kings-chronicles-five-languages-terms.md`

## 2. Settle these first

### Names of God and setting spans

- [ ] **Pronouns for God and Jesus under `deity_pronoun`.** The narrator says
  `{{deity_pronoun:ngài|Ngài}}` (23,114 occ in 5,730 records, 60 books); God
  and Jesus speaking of themselves say `{{deity_pronoun:ta|Ta}}` (15,077 occ in
  3,074 records). Only the capital changes; a pronoun that starts a sentence
  is a plain capital with no span; *Thầy*, *Cha*, *Con* take none.
  Alternatives: always capitalised with no setting; lowercase only.
  Style §6–7.
- [ ] **YHWH is `{{divine_name:CHÚA|Gia-vê}}`** at every level, in capitals,
  particles outside the span (20,655 occ in 5,909 records, every Old
  Testament book but Esther, Ecclesiastes and the Song); the short form *Yah*
  is `{{divine_name:CHÚA|Gia}}` (136 occ, mostly *Hallelu Yah*). Alternatives:
  *Đức Giê-hô-va* (the older Protestant form, not used anywhere); *Gia-vê* as
  the default. Style §7a; Genesis, "The names of God"; Psalms, "Psalm titles and musical terms".
- [ ] **The honorific in *Đức Giê-xu*** is unbracketed even at L (3,723 occ),
  and is dropped in the mouths of those who reject him (John 6:41, 9:11,
  19:19 on Pilate's notice; Acts 26:9). Style §5 and §6.
- [ ] **Christ under `christos`**: `{{christos:Đấng Christ|Đấng Mê-si-a}}`, and
  with the name `Đức Giê-xu {{christos:Christ|Mê-si-a}}` (1,650 occ in 487
  records, 26 books). *Đấng Mê-si-a* plainly, with no span, at John 1:41 and
  4:25; "antichrist" is *kẻ chống Đấng Christ* with no span (1 John 2:18).
  Alternative: *Đấng Mê-si-a* as the default. Style §5 and §7.
- [ ] **Units under `units`**: metric by default and the original unit as the
  alternative, at B and R only; Literal keeps the original unit with no span
  (1,280 occ in 406 records, 30 books). Decimal comma, thousands point.
  Cubit 45 cm, but Ezekiel 40–48 uses the long cubit, 53 cm. This differs
  from the English, which defaults to US units. Style §7; Genesis,
  "Measures"; Prophets §1 "Units in Ezekiel".
- [ ] **"Lord GOD" (*Adonai YHWH*) is *Chúa là CHÚA*** at every level, *Chúa*
  outside the span and never in capitals (924 occ in 301 records; about 200
  times in Ezekiel). Psalms 140:8 and 141:8 have *CHÚA, Chúa của tôi* at L.
  Alternatives: *Chúa CHÚA*, *Chúa Gia-vê*. Psalms, "Settled while drafting";
  Prophets §1.
- [ ] **"The LORD of hosts" is *CHÚA Vạn Quân***, span on *CHÚA* only (783 occ
  in 249 records, 16 books). Alternative: *CHÚA của các đạo quân*. Samuel,
  "Recurring terms"; Psalms, "Settled while drafting".
- [ ] **Gender span for believers**: `{{gender:anh chị em|anh em}}` (577 of the
  701 gender spans); elsewhere *người*, *con cái*, *ai*, *mình* need no span.
  Other spans: `{{gender:con cái|con trai}}`, `{{gender:cha mẹ|cha}}`,
  `{{gender:tổ tiên|tổ phụ}}`. Style §7; Nehemiah, "Settings recap".
- [ ] **Who else takes a span.** The angel of the LORD speaking as God takes
  *Ta* with the span (Genesis 16:10); angels as messengers, the servant of
  Isaiah, the king of Isaiah 9 and 11, the ruler of Micah 5, the Branch and
  "one like a son of man" take none; the king of Psalms 45 and 110 and the
  servant of Matthew 12:18 do, as in the English. About 30 uncertain
  decisions turn on this (section 3).
  Style §7; Isaiah, "Address" (last bullet); Prophets §4.

### Address forms and speech levels

- [ ] **God speaking to people.** *Ta* for himself; to one person *ngươi* (L)
  and *con* (B, R) in calls and promises, *ngươi* at every level in rebukes;
  to the people *các ngươi* at every level, *các con* only where the Hebrew
  calls them children; foreign kings and nations *ngươi*. *Ngươi* is 18,101 occ
  (9,268 at L) and *các ngươi* 7,214. About 55 uncertain decisions turn on
  *ngươi* (section 3). Alternatives: *ngươi* everywhere; *con* everywhere.
  Genesis, "Both languages"; Pentateuch, "Settled while drafting"; Isaiah,
  "Address"; Prophets §3.
- [ ] **Inclusive and exclusive "we".** *Chúng ta* where the hearers are
  included (5,113 occ), *chúng tôi* where they are not (4,954 occ), *chúng
  con* for people praying (1,350 occ). God's "Let us" (Genesis 1:26) is
  *chúng ta*; the eyewitnesses of John 1:14 and 1 John 1:1 are *chúng tôi*.
  About 40 uncertain decisions; no terms doc records the rule, only the
  decisions at Genesis 1:26, 1 Corinthians 1:1 and 1 John 1:1.
- [ ] **Jesus speaking.** *Ta* for himself; *các con* to the disciples, *các
  người* to crowds and opponents; *anh*, *chị*, *ông*, *bà*, *con* to one
  person as age and relation require (Samaritan woman *chị*, the man born
  blind *anh*, Mary Magdalene *bà*, Martha *con*). *Các con* is 4,740 occ, *các
  người* 1,736. Style §6.
- [ ] **Readers of the letters.** Writers say *tôi*, readers are *anh chị em*
  (4,768 occ, with the gender span above where the Greek says "brothers");
  Paul says *ta* and *con* to Timothy and Titus; "Beloved" is *Hỡi những người
  yêu dấu* (L, B) and *Các con thân mến* (R) in 1 John. About 26 uncertain
  decisions (1 Corinthians 1:1, Galatians 1:1, 1 Timothy 1:1, James 1:1,
  1 Peter 1:1). Recorded only in the first decision of each letter.
- [ ] **People praying.** God is *Ngài* or *Chúa*, the one praying *con*, a
  group *chúng con*; "O LORD" is *lạy CHÚA* with no "O"; "Ah, Lord GOD" is
  *Ôi, lạy Chúa là CHÚA*. Job turns to *Chúa* and *con* when he speaks to God
  (uncertain: *tôi* is the alternative, Job 7:7). Isaiah, "Address"; Psalms,
  "All five languages"; Nehemiah, "Address".
- [ ] **Kings and subjects.** Subjects say *vua* (L), *bệ hạ* (B), *đức vua*
  (R) and call themselves *đầy tớ của vua* (L, B) or *tôi* (R); the king says
  *ta* and *ngươi*. *Bệ hạ* is 567 occ, *đức vua* 454. The court words *trẫm*,
  *khanh*, *hoàng thượng* are avoided (Genesis 45:17). Samuel, "Language by
  language"; Kings, "Address forms"; Prophets §3.
- [ ] **People addressing Jesus.** Disciples say *Thầy* (Rabbi, Teacher; 897
  occ, no span) and *chúng con*; believers say *Thưa Chúa* / *Lạy Chúa*; those
  who do not yet believe say *ông*; opponents call him *ông ta*, *người này*.
  Style §6; John 1:38 onward.
- [ ] **Older or younger.** Vietnamese must say which brother or sister is
  older (*anh* / *em*, *chị* / *em gái*), where the Hebrew and Greek do not.
  About 50 uncertain decisions; most are guesses from birth order (Genesis
  13:8, 1 Chronicles 2–8, John 11). Samuel settles Joab as older than
  Abishai, Amnon than Absalom, and Tamar as *em gái* to both; the drafts make
  Aaron older than Moses and Miriam *chị* to both. Genesis, "Address between people"; Samuel, "Settled while drafting";
  Kings, "Before review".
- [ ] **Husband and wife, lovers.** Husband *anh*, wife *em* (B, R); *ông* /
  *bà* at L where the English Literal keeps the formality; Esther calls
  herself *thiếp*; the lovers of the Song say *anh* / *em* and are *chàng* /
  *nàng* in the third person (*nàng* 2,223 occ, *chàng* 471). Genesis,
  "Address between people"; Nehemiah, "Address".
- [ ] **Moses and the prophets speaking to Israel.** Moses says *anh em* and
  *tôi* at B and R, *các ngươi* / *ngươi* at L where the English Literal keeps
  the singular or plural "you"; prophets say *các người* in rebuke and *anh
  em* where they plead. Pentateuch, "Both languages"; Isaiah, "Address".
- [ ] **Generic "you" in Psalms and wisdom.** Proverbs' father says *con*
  (*con ơi*; *hỡi con ta* at L) and *ta* (L) or *cha* (B, R); Psalms 91, 121
  and 128 say *bạn*; the psalmist to his own soul says *mình*; worshippers are
  *các bạn*. 54 uncertain register decisions in Psalms and Proverbs. Psalms,
  "Proverbs" and "Settled while drafting".
- [ ] **Pronouns for the wicked and for nations.** *Hắn* for a hostile man
  (1,630 occ), *nó* for a city, nation or a tribe pictured as one man, *chúng*
  for the guilty and enemies, *họ* for Israel in promises; Zion and the women
  of Ezekiel 16 and 23 are *nàng*. 14 uncertain decisions. Prophets §4
  ("Pronouns for the people").
- [ ] **Angels.** Gabriel and the angels of Daniel and Zechariah say *tôi* and
  *ông*; Daniel and Zechariah answer *thưa chúa tôi* (L, B) and *thưa ngài*
  (R); the guide of Ezekiel 40–48 says *Hỡi con người* and *ông*. 20 uncertain
  decisions. Prophets §3.
- [ ] **Women in narrative.** Mary is *cô* (Matthew 1:18, Luke 1:26), Mary
  Magdalene and the bleeding woman *bà*, the Samaritan woman and the woman of
  John 8 *chị*; a young woman in the law *nàng* (L) / *cô* (B, R). 21
  uncertain decisions; no terms doc records the pattern.

### Key theological terms

- [ ] ***ʿeved* is *đầy tớ*** for God's servants at every level, Literal
  included (2,158 occ); *nô lệ* only for slavery itself (827 occ, half of
  them at L, where the New Testament's *doulos* is *nô lệ*, as Jude 1:1). Kings, "Settled
  while drafting"; Isaiah, "Address"; Psalms, "Settled while drafting".
- [ ] ***Ḥesed* is *tình yêu thương thành tín*** (L, B; 408 occ in 216 records),
  *lòng nhân từ* between people. At R three forms are in use (section 4).
  Alternatives: *lòng nhân từ* for God too; *tình yêu bất biến*. Psalms,
  "Recurring terms"; Genesis, "Recurring terms"; Samuel, "Recurring terms".
- [ ] **Word-for-word key terms at every level**: grace *ân điển* (399 occ),
  truth *chân lý* (317), glory *vinh quang* (884), peace *bình an* (1,005),
  covenant *giao ước* (1,016), eternal life *sự sống đời đời* (134), the world
  *thế gian* (677), disciples *môn đồ* (969), the Word *Ngôi Lời*. Style §5;
  Genesis and Pentateuch, "Recurring terms".
- [ ] **Righteousness** is *sự công chính* (L, B) and *điều công chính* (R)
  (542 occ); "justice" *công lý* (259); the righteous *người công chính*. Style
  §5; Isaiah, "Isaiah"; Prophets §1 (Amos 5:24, Habakkuk 2:4).
- [ ] **Offerings and the sanctuary**: *của lễ thiêu* (766 occ), *tế lễ chuộc
  tội* (363), *tế lễ chuộc lỗi*, *tế lễ bình an*, *tế lễ ngũ cốc* (340); *Lều Hội
  Kiến* (436), *Đền Tạm* (309), *nắp thi ân*, *chuộc tội cho* (648 occ for
  *chuộc tội*). The Pentateuch note asks reviewers to look at these first.
  Pentateuch, "Recurring terms" and "Before review".
- [ ] **The Spirit.** *Đức Thánh Linh* / *Thánh Linh* in the New Testament
  (1,104 occ together); *Thần Linh* in the Old (*Thần Linh của CHÚA*),
  lowercase *thần linh*, *tâm linh* where the Holy Spirit is not meant; the
  Advocate *Đấng Biện Hộ*. 15 uncertain decisions. Style §5; Genesis, "The
  names of God"; Prophets §1.
- [ ] **God's *torah* and the Law.** The Law is *luật pháp* (1,054 occ, lowercase;
  *Luật Pháp* for the book); God's *torah* in the Psalms is *sự dạy dỗ* (L),
  *lời dạy dỗ* (B), *lời dạy* (R), as Psalm 1:2. Style §4; Pentateuch,
  "Recurring terms"; Psalms, "Settled while drafting".
- [ ] **The fear of the LORD**: *kính sợ* (L, B; 615 occ) and *tôn kính* (R;
  276). Psalms, "Recurring terms"; Nehemiah, "Job" and "Ecclesiastes".
- [ ] **"Sign" is *dấu lạ*** (319 occ), never *phép lạ*; *dấu chỉ* is closer
  and is recorded as uncertain at John 2:11. Style §5.
- [ ] **"I am."** John: *Ta là [Đấng Hằng Hữu]* (L), *Ta là Đấng Hằng Hữu*
  (B, R), *Ta Hằng Hữu* (8:58), *Ta đây* (6:20; 18:5–8); Isaiah: *Ta [là] Đấng
  ấy*; Exodus 3:14: *TA LÀ ĐẤNG TA LÀ*. The three do not yet match (section
  4). Style §5 (uncertain); Isaiah, "Formulas" (*ʾani hu*).
- [ ] **Sheol** *Âm Phủ* (L, B) / *nơi của người chết* (R) (197 occ); Abaddon
  *A-ba-đôn* (L), *chốn hủy diệt* (B, R). Psalms, "Recurring terms".
- [ ] **The kingdom**: *vương quốc của Đức Chúa Trời* (185 occ); Matthew's
  *vương quốc thiên đàng* (75 occ, uncertain at Matthew 3:1; the alternatives
  recorded there are *Nước Trời*, *nước thiên đàng* and, at L, *vương quốc
  của các tầng trời*).
- [ ] ***Ḥerem*** is *tận diệt* (131 occ), "devoted thing" *vật bị nguyền để
  hủy diệt* (Joshua, L) and *vật hiến dâng trọn* (Leviticus 27:21, Numbers
  18:14). Joshua–Ruth, "Recurring terms"; Pentateuch, "Settled while drafting".
- [ ] **The skin disease** (*tsaraʿat*) is *bệnh ngoài da nghiêm trọng* (124 occ),
  with *phong* only in a decision. Pentateuch, "Recurring terms".
- [ ] ***Hevel*** in Ecclesiastes is *hơi thở* (L, B), never *hư không*; *ruaḥ*
  as life-breath is *sinh khí*; Qoheleth is *Cô-hê-lết* (L) and *Người Thầy*
  (B, R). Nehemiah, "Ecclesiastes" and "Before review".

### Recurring formulas

- [ ] **The messenger formula**: *CHÚA phán thế này:* (L, 459 occ), *CHÚA
  phán như vầy:* (B, 424), *Đây là lời CHÚA phán:* (R, 382). Samuel,
  "Settled while drafting"; Isaiah, "Formulas".
- [ ] ***Neʾum YHWH***: *CHÚA phán vậy* (L, B; 735 occ) / *CHÚA phán* (R);
  *Chúa là CHÚA phán vậy* for the Lord GOD. Isaiah, "Formulas"; Prophets §1.
- [ ] **Sons of Israel**: *con cháu Y-sơ-ra-ên* at L (537 occ), *dân
  Y-sơ-ra-ên* at B and R (1,577). Pentateuch, "Recurring terms".
- [ ] **Selah and the psalm titles.** *Sê-la* stays at the end of its verse at
  every level (301 occ in 98 records); *Mát-kin*, *Mích-tam*, *Si-ga-giôn* and
  the tune names are kept as names at L and B and explained at R; "For the
  director" *Cho người chỉ huy [ban nhạc]* / *Cho nhạc trưởng* / *Cho người
  hướng dẫn ban nhạc*. 33 uncertain decisions. Psalms, "All five languages" and
  "Psalm titles and musical terms".
- [ ] **"Hỡi con người"** for Ezekiel's "son of man", lowercase, no span (223
  occ in 89 records), kept apart from Jesus' title *Con Người*; "you will know
  that I am the LORD" *các ngươi sẽ biết rằng Ta [là] CHÚA* (L) / *Bấy giờ các
  ngươi sẽ biết rằng Ta là CHÚA* (B) / *Khi ấy các ngươi sẽ biết Ta là CHÚA*
  (R). Prophets §1 "Ezekiel".
- [ ] **The word-event formula**: *Lời CHÚA đến với tôi rằng:* / *Lời CHÚA
  đến với tôi:* / *CHÚA ban cho tôi sứ điệp này:* (about 200 occ). Kings, "The
  regnal formulas"; Isaiah, "Formulas".
- [ ] **The regnal formulas of Kings and Chronicles**: *làm điều ác trước mắt
  CHÚA* (L) / *trước mặt CHÚA* (B) / *làm điều CHÚA cho là ác* (R); death
  *nằm xuống với tổ phụ mình* / *an giấc cùng tổ tiên* / *qua đời*; the source
  book *Sách Lịch Sử Các Vua Y-sơ-ra-ên*, never *Sử Ký*. Kings, "The regnal
  formulas".
- [ ] ***Hoy***: *Khốn cho* (L, B; 159 occ) / *Thật khốn khổ cho* (R); the
  funeral cry and Lamentations' *ʾekhah* are *Than ôi*. Isaiah, "Formulas";
  Prophets, "Lamentations".
- [ ] **"Truly, truly"**: *Thật vậy, thật vậy, Ta nói với...* (L, B in John)
  and *Ta nói thật với...* (R; 76 records, nearly all in the Gospels). Style
  §5.
- [ ] ***Massa***: *sấm ngôn* (L, B) / *sứ điệp* (R); *gánh nặng* at every
  level in Jeremiah 23:33–40 for the wordplay. Isaiah, "Formulas".
- [ ] **Oaths**: *Thật như CHÚA hằng sống* (120 occ); *Nguyện Đức Chúa Trời
  phạt tôi nặng nề*; *Thật như Ta hằng sống* for God. Samuel, "Recurring
  terms"; Prophets §1.

### Names and spelling conventions

- [ ] **New Old Testament names** follow the usual Protestant transliteration
  and stay the same everywhere; published forms win. About 20 uncertain name
  decisions (Numbers 33:18, Acts 27:2, Romans 16:1), and three names spelled
  two ways (section 4). Genesis, Pentateuch, Joshua–Ruth, Samuel, Kings,
  Nehemiah and Prophets, "Names".
- [ ] **Names a reviewer is asked to confirm**: *E-xơ-ra* (Ezra) beside
  *Ê-xơ-ra* (1 Chronicles 4:17); *Giô-suê* the high priest beside Ezra's
  *Giê-sua*; *Cô-nia*, *Nê-bu-cát-rết-sa* (Nebuchadrezzar) beside
  *Nê-bu-cát-nết-sa*; *Ma-gốc* (Genesis) beside Revelation's *Ma-gót*;
  *MÊ-NÊ, MÊ-NÊ, TÊ-KEN và PHÁC-SIN*. Isaiah, "Names"; Prophets §2 and §5.
- [ ] **Capitals for titles**: *Đấng Thánh của Y-sơ-ra-ên*, *Đấng Cứu Chuộc*,
  *Chồi*, *Con Người*, *Đấng Tiên Tri*, *Đầy Tớ* for Jesus in Acts 3:13;
  lowercase for the servant of Isaiah 53 (*người*) and "my messenger" in
  Malachi 3:1. Style §4; Isaiah, "Formulas" and "Isaiah".
- [ ] **Tone marks and encoding**: *hòa*, *thủy*, *khỏe* (the older placement),
  stored NFC. Style §4.

### Style

- [ ] **Quotation marks and speech**: straight double quotes, single inside;
  speech introduced with a colon (`Đức Giê-xu nói: "..."`; 15,314 occ); God's
  oracles and the prophets' poetry take no quotation marks. Style §4 and §6;
  Prophets §4 "Speech".
- [ ] **Aspect and brackets by level**: no articles; *đã*, *đang*, *sẽ* only
  where the sense needs them, never bracketed; the historic present kept at L
  by leaving out *đã*; square brackets for supplied words at L only (15,756
  at L; at B and R only the bracketed textual variants of Mark 16:9–20 and
  John 7:53–8:11). Style §3;
  Prophets §4 "Brackets".
- [ ] **Readable register**: short sentences, plain words, fewer
  Sino-Vietnamese terms (*sứ điệp* over *sấm ngôn*, *người cai trị* over *thủ
  lĩnh*); explains only what the English Readable explains. Style §3;
  Prophets §4 "Readable".
- [ ] **Numbers**: in words in running text, figures from 100 and for list
  totals, measures in figures with a decimal comma. Not yet applied evenly
  (section 4). Style §4; Nehemiah, "All five languages"; Prophets §4.
- [ ] **Lines**: `\q` breaks only where the English level has them, at
  Vietnamese phrase boundaries; a Hebrew sentence that runs across verses is
  closed in each Vietnamese verse, with a decision once per block. Style §7a;
  Psalms, "Settled while drafting".
- [ ] **Clock time**: L keeps the ancient count (*khoảng giờ thứ mười*), B and R
  give the modern hour (*khoảng bốn giờ chiều*). Style §4.

## 3. Recurring uncertain choices

`vi-uncertain.md` lists 964 decisions in 60 books: 635 word choices, 277
register (address) choices, 25 supplied words, 19 setting choices and 8
others. The largest books are Psalms (110), John (71), Numbers (53), Genesis
(52) and Exodus (48). Grouped by what they decide, these choices recur; each
group can be settled once and then applied to all its verses.

- [ ] ***Ngươi* against *con*, *anh em* or *ông*** (55): who is spoken to as
