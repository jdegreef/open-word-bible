- [ ] [LEV 23:36](https://openwordbible.org/sw/lev/23/#v36) L/B/R · word_choice · "mkutano wa heshima" (L, B); "mkutano maalum" (R) — 'Atseret is a gathering on a day when work is held back. "Kusanyiko" is kept for qahal, so the word is "mkutano", as for the holy convocation, with "wa heshima" (solemn); Readable says "mkutano maalum", as the English says "special gathering".

## Hesabu (13)

- [ ] [NUM 3:50](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/3/#v50) B/R · setting_alternative · {{units:karibu kilo 15.7 za fedha...|shekeli 1,365...}} — 1,365 shekels at 11.5 g each come to about 15.7 kg, the Pentateuch's metric value; the English "34 pounds" rounds the same weight. The shekels are the Measures setting.
- [ ] [NUM 4:10](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/4/#v10) L/B/R · word_choice · "machela" — The carrying frame (m1) was a pole or a frame of poles borne by men; "machela" is a frame carried between people, the nearest everyday Swahili word. Also in verse 12.
- [ ] [NUM 7:14](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/7/#v14) L/B/R · word_choice · "kisahani" (R "kisahani kidogo") — The kaf is a small shallow dish shaped like a palm (m1), so Swahili says "kisahani", a small dish, rather than "kijiko" (spoon), which names a different object.
- [ ] [NUM 11:17](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/11/#v17) L/B/R · word_choice · "Roho aliye juu yako", with personal agreement ("aliye", "kumweka") — The English capitalises Spirit because verse 29 calls it Yahweh's Spirit (r-en-1). - [ ] [NUM 13:11](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/13/#v11) L/B/R · word_choice · "Gaddi" with a double d for the man's name — "Gadi" is already the Swahili name of the tribe of Gad (verse 15). - [ ] [NUM 13:21](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/13/#v21) L/B/R · word_choice · "Zini" for the wilderness of Zin — Swahili usually writes the Hebrew ts as "s", but "jangwa la Sini" is already the wilderness of Sin in Exodus 16, a different place. - [ ] [NUM 15:19](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/15/#v19) L/B/R · word_choice · "kuinua toleo" (L); "kutenga toleo" (B); "kutenga sehemu kuwa zawadi" (R) — The portion lifted off a larger amount for the LORD (m1). - [ ] [NUM 24:22](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/24/#v22) L/B/R · word_choice · "Kaini"; Readable keeps "Ashuru" — The Kenites' name is the same Hebrew word as Cain's, and Swahili writes it "Kaini"; Readable says "watu wa Kaini". Where the English Readable says "Assyria", Swahili keeps "Ashuru", which is already the usual Swahili name of Assyria.
- [ ] [NUM 25:4](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/25/#v4) L/B · word_choice · "ukawatundike juu ya nguzo" (hang them on poles) — Swahili has no everyday verb for impaling. "Kuwatundika juu ya nguzo" names the public display of the bodies (m1) without the Christian associations of "kusulubisha" (to crucify).
- [ ] [NUM 25:11](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/25/#v11) L/B/R · word_choice · "wivu" at every level — Where the English Balanced and Readable say "zeal", Swahili keeps "wivu": used of God it is the protective passion of "Mungu mwenye wivu" (Exodus 20:5), not envy, and it keeps the link with that phrase. "Bidii" and "ari" would say only effort or eagerness.
- [ ] [NUM 29:1](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/29/#v1) L/B/R · word_choice · "siku ya mlio mkuu" (L); "milio mikubwa" (B, R) — "Mlio" is the sound of a horn, bell or signal and leaves the instrument unnamed, as the Hebrew does and as in Leviticus 23:24; a reviewer may prefer naming the horn.
- [ ] [NUM 29:35](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/29/#v35) L/B · word_choice · "mkutano wa heshima" — As Leviticus 23:36: the closing assembly is kept apart from "mkutano mtakatifu" (holy convocation) and from "kusanyiko" (the assembly, qahal); a reviewer may prefer another term.
- [ ] [NUM 30:7](https://openwordbible.org/sw/num/30/#v7) L/B/R · word_choice · "tamko la haraka" (L); "ahadi ya haraka" (B); "ahadi aliyoitoa bila kufikiri" (R) — "Ya haraka" (hasty) names a promise spoken in haste without blaming the woman, as the Hebrew word for rash speech does.
