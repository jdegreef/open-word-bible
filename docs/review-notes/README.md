# Review notes

Notes for the people who review the translation. They are not part of the
translation, and every record stays `ai_draft` until a named reviewer approves it.

## Where a native reviewer starts

Each language has two files:

- `<lang>-review-checklist.md`: start here. It lists, first, the choices that
  decide the wording of many verses at once (names of God and the setting spans,
  address forms, key terms, formulas, name spellings, style), then the choices the
  drafters were unsure of most often, the places where books disagree with each
  other, and the passages to read closely against well-known translations.
- `<lang>-uncertain.md`: every rendering decision in the language that the
  drafters marked uncertain, in book order, each linked to its verse. It is
  generated by `python3 scripts/review_checklist.py <lang> --write`; regenerate it
  after review work, and decisions that are no longer uncertain drop out.

| Language | Checklist | Every uncertain decision |
|---|---|---|
| Spanish | [es-review-checklist.md](es-review-checklist.md) | [es-uncertain.md](es-uncertain.md) |
| French | [fr-review-checklist.md](fr-review-checklist.md) | [fr-uncertain.md](fr-uncertain.md) |
| Swahili | [sw-review-checklist.md](sw-review-checklist.md) | [sw-uncertain.md](sw-uncertain.md) |
| Vietnamese | [vi-review-checklist.md](vi-review-checklist.md) | [vi-uncertain.md](vi-uncertain.md) |
| Korean | [ko-review-checklist.md](ko-review-checklist.md) | [ko-uncertain.md](ko-uncertain.md) |

A choice that is settled differently is changed in its terms doc (`docs/*terms*.md`),
and every passage drafted under it is revised to match.
