- [ ] [JAS 3:6](https://openwordbible.org/hi/jas/3/#v6) L · word_choice · "जीवन के चक्र" — The wheel of existence is the whole course of human life (meaning decision m3). "चक्र" keeps the picture of the wheel; Balanced and Readable say "जीवन की पूरी गति" and "पूरे जीवन".
- [ ] [JAS 3:15](https://openwordbible.org/hi/jas/3/#v15) L · word_choice · "शारीरिक" for psychikos — The word means belonging to human life without God's Spirit (meaning decision m2). "स्वाभाविक" in Hindi now means simply natural or normal, with no edge; "शारीरिक" (of the flesh, worldly) gives the contrast. Balanced and Readable say it plainly.
- [ ] [JAS 4:4](https://openwordbible.org/hi/jas/4/#v4) L/B · word_choice · "हे व्यभिचारिणियो ... जानतीं" (L); "हे विश्वासघाती लोगो" (B) — Literal keeps the Greek feminine plural, and so the feminine verb. The picture is God's people as an unfaithful wife (meaning decision m1). "व्यभिचारी लोग" in Balanced would sound like a charge of literal adultery, so Balanced says "विश्वासघाती", unfaithful; Readable explains the picture.
- [ ] [JAS 4:5](https://openwordbible.org/hi/jas/4/#v5) L/B/R · word_choice · "आत्मा" — Hindi has no capital letter to mark the English lowercase "spirit", but "आत्मा" with no "पवित्र" leaves open, as the English does, whether the human spirit or the Holy Spirit is meant (meaning decision m4). Readable gives "आत्मा" feminine agreement ("रखी"), as for the human spirit.
- [ ] [JAS 5:6](https://openwordbible.org/hi/jas/5/#v6) L/B/R · register · plain "वह ... करता" at every level — The subject may be the murdered righteous one or God (meaning decision m2). The honorific would decide for God, so every level keeps the plain form that goes with the righteous one of the sentence before, as the English keeps a plain "he".

## 1 पतरस (5)

- [ ] [1PE 1:24](https://openwordbible.org/hi/1pe/1/#v24) L · word_choice · "सब देहधारी" for "all flesh" — "सब शरीर" would read as "the whole body" in Hindi. "देहधारी" (those who have a body of flesh) keeps the picture of flesh and means all human beings in their mortality (meaning decision m2); Balanced and Readable say "मनुष्य" and "लोग".
- [ ] [1PE 3:3](https://openwordbible.org/hi/1pe/3/#v3) L/B/R · word_choice · "छिपा हुआ मनुष्यत्व" (L); "छिपा हुआ व्यक्तित्व" (B) — The hidden person of the heart is the inner self (meaning decision m4). "मनुष्य" spoken to wives would sound odd in Hindi; Literal keeps the root of the Greek word in "मनुष्यत्व", Balanced says "व्यक्तित्व", as the English says "self".
- [ ] [1PE 3:10](https://openwordbible.org/hi/1pe/3/#v10) L/B · word_choice · "मेल" (L, B); "शान्ति" (R) — Peace here is peace with others (meaning decision m4), which Hindi says with "मेल", as in "मेल करानेवाले" (James 3:18). Readable keeps the key term "शान्ति".
- [ ] [1PE 4:3](https://openwordbible.org/hi/1pe/4/#v3) L/B/R · word_choice · "अन्यजातियों" (L); "विधर्मियों" (B); "परमेश्वर को न जाननेवाले लोग" (R) — Literal keeps the key term for Gentiles. The readers were mostly Gentiles themselves, so Balanced and Readable say what is meant, the pagan world (meaning decision m1), as in 2:12; "ग़ैर-यहूदी" would mislead.
- [ ] [1PE 4:4](https://openwordbible.org/hi/1pe/4/#v4) L/B · word_choice · "उच्छृंखलता" (L), "उच्छृंखल ... जीवन" (B) for asotia — Asotia is reckless, wasteful living. "लुचपन" is kept for aselgeia (sensuality, verse 3), as in Jude, so this word gets its own; Balanced and Readable spell out the wild and wasteful life, as the English does.

## 2 पतरस (1)

- [ ] [2PE 2:4](https://openwordbible.org/hi/2pe/2/#v4) L/B/R · word_choice · "भक्तिहीन" (asebes); "नियमहीन" (athesmos); "अधर्म के काम" (anomos); "अन्यायी", "दुष्ट" (adikos) — Four Greek words for the wicked stand in this sentence. "भक्तिहीन" and "अधर्म" are the words Jude uses for ungodly and lawless; the other two get their own Hindi words so that the reader can tell them apart. Readable says "those who do not honour God", as the English does.

## प्रकाशितवाक्य (22)

- [ ] [REV 1:10](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/1/#v10) L/B/R · word_choice · "पुस्तक" (L, B); "किताब" (R) — The Greek word is the ordinary word for a book, which then took the form of a scroll (m3). Hindi has no living word for a scroll, so the plain word for a book is used through Revelation; where the rolled form matters (5:1; 6:14) the sentence says it is rolled.
- [ ] [REV 1:14](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/1/#v14) L/B/R · word_choice · "उत्तम काँसा" (L, B); "काँसा" (R) — A highly polished bronze alloy (m2). "काँसा" is bronze; "पीतल" is brass. The same word in 2:18.
- [ ] [REV 1:20](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/1/#v20) L/B/R · word_choice · "स्वर्गदूत" for the angels of the seven churches — The Greek word can mean any messenger, and some readers take the angels of the churches to be human leaders or messengers. Hindi keeps "स्वर्गदूत", the word used for angels everywhere else in the book, because the meaning decision (m3) reads them as heavenly beings who stand for each church. The …
- [ ] [REV 2:13](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/2/#v13) L/B/R · word_choice · "अन्तिपास" — A name keeps its Bible spelling, with the nasal consonant, as the style guide allows for names (section 7b).
- [ ] [REV 2:20](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/2/#v20) L/B/R · word_choice · "ईज़ेबेल" — The Hindi Bible form of the queen's name (m1), with the nukta.
- [ ] [REV 3:7](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/3/#v7) L/B/R · word_choice · "फिलदिलफिया" — The established Hindi Bible spelling of the city, written without the nukta as names keep their Bible form (style guide, section 5).
- [ ] [REV 3:14](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/3/#v14) L/B · word_choice · "मूल" (beginning) — The source and origin of creation (m3), not its first creature; "मूल" says source. Readable says everything began in him.
- [ ] [REV 4:1](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/4/#v1) L/B/R · word_choice · "यशब", "माणिक्य", "पन्ना" — Precious stones whose exact colors are uncertain (m5); the Hindi names of the nearest stones.
- [ ] [REV 4:4](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/4/#v4) L/B/R · word_choice · "पुरनिए" (L); "प्राचीन अगुवे" (B, R) — Literal keeps the word for elders used in the Gospels and Acts. Balanced and Readable say "प्राचीन अगुवे", not the Gospels' "बुज़ुर्ग अगुवे" (village or council elders) or Acts' "कलीसिया के अगुवे": these are heavenly beings who lead the worship (m1). - [ ] [REV 5:5](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/5/#v5) L/B/R · register · honorific forms for the Lion and the Lamb ("वे ... खोल सकते हैं", "उन्होंने") — The Lion and the Lamb are Jesus (m1, and 5:6), who takes the honorific third person when the elders and the narrator speak of him (style guide, section 4). Literal keeps the bare verb "जयवंत हुआ है" of the title. The same through the book.
- [ ] [REV 5:9](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/5/#v9) L/B/R · register · "आप" to the Lamb — The hymn is sung to the Lamb, the risen Jesus, and prayer to the risen Jesus says "आप", as in Acts (style guide, section 7b); hymns to God say "तू" (4:11).
- [ ] [REV 5:11](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/5/#v11) L/B · word_choice · "लाखों-लाखों" for myriads of myriads — A myriad is ten thousand; Hindi counts large numbers in lakhs, and "लाखों-लाखों" gives the same sense of a number beyond counting (Daniel 7:10) without an unfamiliar unit.
- [ ] [REV 6:8](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/6/#v8) L/B/R · word_choice · "महामारी" (L, B); "बीमारी" (R) — The second "death" in the list means deadly disease, as in Ezekiel's four judgments (m3); English Literal keeps "death", but Hindi "मृत्यु" twice in one sentence would hide the sense, so every level says plague.
- [ ] [REV 7:4](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/7/#v4) L/B/R · word_choice · "1,44,000" in Indian digit grouping — Hindi writes figures in international digits (style guide, section 5) but groups them in lakhs, as Hindi readers do; the number is the same as the English 144,000.
- [ ] [REV 8:11](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/8/#v11) L/B/R · word_choice · "नागदौना" (Wormwood) — The Hindi name of the bitter plant (m1), used as the star's name.
- [ ] [REV 9:11](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/9/#v11) L/B/R · word_choice · "अबद्दोन", "अपुल्लयोन" — The two names, Destruction and Destroyer (m1), kept as names in their Hindi Bible forms; Readable gives the meaning, as the English Readable does.
- [ ] [REV 9:16](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/9/#v16) L/B/R · word_choice · "दस हज़ार गुणा दस हज़ार का दुगना" (L); "20,00,00,000" (B, R) — Literal spells out the Greek "two myriads of myriads"; Balanced and Readable give the figure, as the English does, in the Indian grouping (twenty crore, the English 200,000,000).

- [ ] [REV 14:1](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/14/#v1) L/B/R · setting_alternative · No deity_pronoun spans ("उनके साथ", "उनका नाम") — Devanagari has no capital letters, so the setting has nothing to change (style guide, section 7). - [ ] [REV 14:1](https://openwordbible.org/hi/rev/14/#v1) L/B/R · word_choice · "1,44,000", with Indian digit grouping — Large numbers are grouped in the Indian way (lakh, thousand), as in Revelation 1-11; a reviewer should confirm this for figures in running text.
