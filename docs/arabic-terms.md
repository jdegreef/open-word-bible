# Arabic terms: Genesis to Revelation

**Status: drafting glossary, awaiting native Arabic reviewers.** Every Arabic
record is `ai_draft`. The forms below are the forms drafters use, in every
book; if a choice here changes, every passage drafted under it is revised.

## How this glossary relates to the style guide

`docs/arabic-style.md` is the brief for drafting Arabic and holds in full:
the levels, the register and vowel marks, address and the dual, the divine
name, the reader settings, the key terms (section 7), Christian Arabic and
Islamic usage (section 9), typography, the recurring formulas (section 12)
and the book-by-book workflow (section 14). This glossary is the single list
of recurring words and names, so that two books drafted in parallel spell
the same person and say the same thing the same way. Each book's own terms
note (`docs/isaiah-terms.md` and the others) still settles the meaning; this
file settles only the Arabic wording.

- **One form per person, place and term.** A drafter who needs a name or a
  recurring word not listed here forms it by the rules of the style guide,
  section 6, records it in a decision where it first appears, and adds it
  to "Added while drafting" at the end of this file in the same pull
  request.
- A form marked **(L)**, **(B)** or **(R)** applies at that level only;
  otherwise it holds at every level.
- The names are the established forms of the Arabic church; they are
  conventions, not the wording of any version, and may be used. Names are
  written without vowel marks; a shadda is written where the established
  form has one (*متّى*, *حنّة*, *يسّى*).
- Never type Greek or Hebrew: the source words are given in transliteration.
