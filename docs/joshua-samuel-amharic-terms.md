# Amharic: Joshua to 2 Samuel

**Status: proposed, awaiting a native Amharic reviewer.** The choices used to
draft Joshua, Judges, Ruth and 1–2 Samuel in Amharic, so that books drafted
in parallel read as one translation. `docs/amharic-style.md` and
`docs/pentateuch-amharic-terms.md` still hold, including its section on what
Genesis to Deuteronomy settled; this list adds what these books need.
Decision text stays in English, quoting the Amharic words.

## Carried over from the Pentateuch

- The Amharic Genesis to Deuteronomy is on main (`content/GEN/` …
  `content/DEU/`). Names, places and recurring words keep the form used
  there: grep it before choosing a new one.
- "The LORD your God" puts the name second: «አምላክህ
  {{divine_name:እግዚአብሔር|ያህዌ}}», with a prefix on both words
  («ለአምላካችሁ {{divine_name:ለእግዚአብሔር|ለያህዌ}}»).
- Inheritance (*naḥalah*) is ርስት; possession (*achuzzah*, *yerushah*) is
  ይዞታ; to take possession (*yarash*) is መውረስ. *Ḥerem* is ለጥፋት መለየት.
- Nations (*goyim*) are ሕዝቦች; አሕዛብ only where the New Testament's
  Amharic quotes the verse with it.
- "Here I am": እነሆኝ (L); «አቤት» (B, R).

## Address

- Subjects, servants and soldiers speak to a king with እርስዎ, ጌታዬ ንጉሥ, and
  call themselves ባሪያህ (L) / አገልጋይዎ (B, R) where the Hebrew uses the
  courtly third person. David speaks so to Saul, Abigail to David.
- A prophet bringing God's word to a king says አንተ (Samuel to Saul, Nathan
  to David), as Moses to Pharaoh.
- Kings speak down with አንተ / እናንተ. Friends and equals say አንተ (David and
  Jonathan). Family members say አንተ / አንቺ (Naomi and Ruth).
- Ruth speaks to Boaz with እርስዎ; he answers with አንቺ and ልጄ.

## Poetry and parallels

- Keep each level's `\q` breaks where the English has them: the Song of
  Deborah (Judges 5), Hannah's prayer (1 Samuel 2:1–10), David's lament
  (2 Samuel 1:19–27), his song (2 Samuel 22, which is Psalm 18) and last
  words (23:1–7).
- New Testament quotations keep the Amharic New Testament's wording where
  the Hebrew agrees: 1 Samuel 13:14 (Acts 13:22); 2 Samuel 7:14 (Hebrews
  1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18); 22:3 (Hebrews 2:13); 22:50 (Romans 15:9).
- Ruth 4:18–22's genealogy keeps the names of Matthew 1:3–6 in the Amharic
  New Testament (ሰልሞን, ቦዔዝ, ኢዮቤድ, እሴይ), except where the Greek form differs
  from the Hebrew: Hezron stays ኤስሮን, as in Genesis 46, where Matthew has
  ኤስሮም.
