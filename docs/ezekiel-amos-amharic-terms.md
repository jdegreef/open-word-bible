# Amharic: Ezekiel, Daniel, Hosea, Joel and Amos

**Status: proposed, awaiting a native Amharic reviewer.** The choices used to
draft these five books in Amharic, so that books drafted in parallel read as
one translation. `docs/amharic-style.md` and the earlier terms lists still
hold, above all the prophets table of
`docs/ecclesiastes-lamentations-amharic-terms.md` (Isaiah and Jeremiah):
"Thus says the LORD", "declares the LORD", woe, "in that day", oracle, the
Holy One of Israel, the remnant. This list adds what these books need.
Decision text stays in English, quoting the Amharic words.

## Carried over

- Genesis to Lamentations are drafted in Amharic (on main or in their open
  pull requests; the drafting brief names the paths). Names, places and
  recurring words keep the forms used there: grep before choosing a new one.
- Adonai YHWH ("the Lord GOD") is «ጌታ {{divine_name:እግዚአብሔር|ያህዌ}}»;
  "declares the Lord GOD" «ይላል ጌታ {{dn}}» after the words; "Thus says the
  Lord GOD" «ጌታ {{dn}} እንዲህ ይላል፦». YHWH Tsevaot L «{{dn}} ጸባኦት», B, R
  «የሠራዊት አምላክ {{dn}}». Suffixes go after `}}`.
- Nebuchadnezzar ናቡከደነፆር (also where Ezekiel's Hebrew spells it with r).

## Daniel

- **2:4b–7:28 are Aramaic.** Translate from the Aramaic as the source gives
  it; terms and names are the same as in the Hebrew chapters.
- Names: Daniel ዳንኤል; Belteshazzar ብልጣሶር; Hananiah, Mishael, Azariah
  ሐናንያ, ሚሳኤል, ዓዛርያስ; Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego ሲድራቅ, ሚሳቅ, አብደናጎ;
  Belshazzar ቤልሻዛር (kept apart from ብልጣሶር); Darius the Mede ሜዶናዊው
  ዳርዮስ; Cyrus ቂሮስ; Gabriel ገብርኤል and Michael ሚካኤል (as Luke 1 and
  Revelation 12 on main); Ashpenaz አስፋናዝ.
- "One like a son of man" (7:13): L «የሰው ልጅ የሚመስል», B the English B; R
  the English R ("someone who looked like a human being"); a decision names
  Matthew 24:30, 26:64 and Revelation 1:13.
- The Ancient of Days: L ዘመናት የሸመገለው (B, R the English wording); the
  Most High ልዑል; the holy ones ቅዱሳን; the abomination that desolates
  (9:27; 11:31; 12:11): keep Matthew 24:15's Amharic where the Hebrew
  agrees.
- Satraps ጠቅላይ ገዢዎች; governors አገረ ገዦች (as Esther and Ezra); "O king,
  live forever" «ንጉሥ ሆይ፣ ለዘላለም ይኑሩ» (subjects say እርስዎ to the king).

## Hosea, Joel, Amos

- Hosea: Gomer daughter of Diblaim የዴብላይም ልጅ ጎሜር; Jezreel ኢይዝራኤል;
  Lo-ruhamah, Lo-ammi: follow the English (names or meanings) and keep
  Romans 9:25–26 and 1 Peter 2:10 where the Hebrew agrees; Ephraim ኤፍሬም
  (for Israel); "return" ተመለሰ; ḥesed ጽኑ ፍቅር; knowledge of God እግዚአብሔርን
  ማወቅ. NT: 6:6 (Matthew 9:13), 10:8 (Luke 23:30), 11:1 (Matthew 2:15),
  13:14 (1 Corinthians 15:55).
- Joel: the day of the LORD የ{{dn}} ቀን; locust words as Exodus 10 and
  Leviticus 11 on main; 2:28–32 (Hebrew 3:1–5) = Acts 2:17–21 and Romans
  10:13: keep the Amharic of Acts where the Hebrew agrees with the Greek.
- Amos: "For three transgressions of X, and for four" one formula through
  chapters 1–2; the shepherds of Tekoa ቴቁሔ; 5:24 drafted fresh; NT: 5:25–27
  (Acts 7:42–43), 9:11–12 (Acts 15:16–17). Joel 3:16 (Hebrew 4:16) = Amos
  1:2: one wording.
