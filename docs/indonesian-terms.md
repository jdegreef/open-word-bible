# Indonesian terms: Genesis to Revelation

**Status: drafting glossary, awaiting a native Indonesian reviewer.** Every
Indonesian record is `ai_draft`. The forms below are the forms drafters use,
in every book; if a choice here changes, every passage drafted under it is
revised.

## How this glossary relates to the style guide

`docs/indonesian-style.md` is the brief for drafting Indonesian and holds in
full: the levels, the register, address, the divine name and pronouns for
God, the reader settings, the key terms (section 7), the Arabic-derived
vocabulary rules (section 9), typography, the recurring formulas (section
12a) and the book-by-book workflow (section 12b). This glossary is the
single list of recurring words and names, so that two books drafted in
parallel spell the same person and say the same thing the same way. Each
book's own terms note (`docs/isaiah-terms.md` and the others) still settles
the meaning; this file settles only the Indonesian wording.

- **One form per person, place and term.** A drafter who needs a name or a
  recurring word not listed here forms it by the rules of the style guide,
  section 6, records it in a decision where it first appears, and adds it
  to "Added while drafting" at the end of this file in the same pull
  request.
- A form marked **(L)**, **(B)** or **(R)** applies at that level only;
  otherwise it holds at every level.
- The names are the established forms of the Indonesian church; they are
  conventions, not the wording of any version, and may be used. Spelling
  follows EYD and KBBI.
- Never type Greek or Hebrew: the source words are given in transliteration.
