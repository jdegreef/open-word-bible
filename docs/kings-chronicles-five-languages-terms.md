# 1 and 2 Kings and 1 and 2 Chronicles in French, Spanish, Swahili, Vietnamese and Korean

**Status: proposed, awaiting native reviewers.** These are the choices used to
draft Kings and Chronicles in five languages, so that chapter blocks drafted
in parallel read as one translation in each language. They continue
`docs/samuel-five-languages-terms.md` (1 and 2 Samuel, drafted just before),
whose tables, names and address forms all hold here and are not repeated,
and the notes it lists (the Pentateuch note for French, Spanish and Swahili;
the Genesis note for Vietnamese and Korean; the Psalms note for all five).
The English choices are in `docs/samuel-chronicles-terms.md`. Each column
was checked against the forms the language already publishes; where they
differed, the published form won.

If a choice here changes, every passage drafted under it is revised.
Decision text stays in English, quoting the words of the language.

## Order and parallels

- **Kings first, then Chronicles.** Chronicles retells Samuel and Kings.
  Where its Hebrew is the same as the earlier book's, Chronicles reuses the
  language's Samuel or Kings wording at each level; where the Hebrew differs,
  the wording differs, and a rendering decision is added only where the
  language made its own choice (the meaning decisions already note the
  Hebrew's).
- **Psalm parallels:** 1 Chronicles 16:8–36 reuses each language's published
  Psalms 105:1–15, 96 and 106:1, 47–48.
- **2 Kings 18:13–20:19** is Isaiah 36–39, which has no translation in these
  languages yet: draft it once here, and Isaiah will reuse it.
- **1 Chronicles 1–9** are genealogies: keep each list formula identical ("the
  sons of X: A, B and C"; "X fathered Y") and the decisions short. Every name
  stays, in the language's usual form.

## All five languages

The Samuel note's rules hold: translate from the Hebrew with the gloss, the
English and the meaning decisions, never from a reference Bible; lines follow
the English record (`\q` only where the English level has it, such as the
oracle against Sennacherib in 2 Kings 19:21–28 and the psalm of 1 Chronicles
16; Solomon's prayer is prose); speech marks, address forms (*tu*/*tú* to the
king, no *usted*) and the king's officials as in Samuel; Spanish `plural_you`
for every plural "you"; units where the English converts (cubit 45 cm, shekel
11.5 g, talent 34 kg, bath 22 litres, cor 220 litres). Every SEIGNEUR, SEÑOR,
BWANA, CHÚA and 주 standing for YHWH in the tables below is the `divine_name`
span of the earlier notes (Korean particles stay outside it:
`{{divine_name:주|야훼}}께서`). Zero to two rendering decisions per sentence;
a book-wide choice once per block of chapters.

Narration follows each style guide: French passé simple at L and B and
**passé composé at R** (so the R formulas below are in the passé composé);
Spanish preterite; Swahili *-li-* for the first verb, then *-ka-*; Vietnamese
plain narration; Korean plain written style in 했다 (not 하였다).

## The regnal formulas

One wording per level in each language, through all four books. L follows the
Hebrew; B is natural; R may split the sentence.

**Accession (Israel).** "In the Nth year of X king of Judah, Y son of Z became
king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned N years."
- French: L *En la Ne année de X, roi de Juda, Y fils de Z régna sur Israël à
  Samarie N ans*; B *La Ne année du règne de X, roi de Juda, Y, fils de Z,
  devint roi d’Israël à Samarie ; il régna N ans*; R *... Y est devenu roi
  d’Israël à Samarie, et il a régné N ans.*
- Spanish: *En el año N de X, rey de Judá, Y hijo de Z comenzó a reinar sobre
  Israel en Samaria, y reinó N años.*
- Swahili: *Katika mwaka wa N wa X mfalme wa Yuda, Y mwana wa Z alianza
  kutawala juu ya Israeli huko Samaria, akatawala miaka N.*
- Vietnamese: *Năm thứ N đời X vua Giu-đa, Y con trai Z lên làm vua Y-sơ-ra-ên
  tại Sa-ma-ri và trị vì N năm.*
- Korean: L 유다 왕 X의 제N년에 ...; B 유다 왕 X가 다스린 지 N년째 되는 해에 Z의
  아들 Y가 사마리아에서 이스라엘의 왕이 되어 N년 동안 다스렸다.

**Accession (Judah).** "He was N years old when he became king, and he reigned
N years in Jerusalem."
- French *Il avait N ans quand il devint roi, et il régna N ans à Jérusalem*
  (R *est devenu ... a régné*); Spanish *Tenía N años cuando comenzó a
  reinar, y reinó N años en Jerusalén*; Swahili L *[alikuwa] mwana wa miaka N
  alipoanza kutawala*, B *alikuwa na umri wa miaka N alipoanza kutawala, naye
  akatawala miaka N huko Yerusalemu*; Vietnamese *X được N tuổi khi lên làm
  vua và trị vì N năm tại Giê-ru-sa-lem*; Korean X는 N세에 왕이 되어
  예루살렘에서 N년 동안 다스렸다.

**His mother.** French L *Le nom de sa mère [était] ...*, B, R *Sa mère
s’appelait ...*; Spanish *Su madre se llamaba ...*; Swahili L *Na jina la mama
yake [lilikuwa] ...*, B, R *Mama yake aliitwa ... binti wa ...*; Vietnamese
*Mẹ vua tên là ...*; Korean 그의 어머니의 이름은 ...(이)었다.

**The sin of Jeroboam.** French *marcha dans la voie de Jéroboam ... et dans
le péché qu’il avait fait commettre à Israël*; Spanish L *anduvo en el camino
de Jeroboam ... con que hizo pecar a Israel*, B *siguió el camino de Jeroboam
y el pecado que este hizo cometer a Israel*; Swahili *alienda katika njia ya
Yeroboamu ... na katika dhambi yake aliyowafanya Israeli kutenda*; Vietnamese
*đi theo đường lối của Giê-rô-bô-am ... tội mà ông đã khiến Y-sơ-ra-ên phạm*;
Korean 여로보암의 길로 걸으며 그가 이스라엘에게 짓게 한 죄를 따랐다.

**The sources.** "The rest of the acts of X ... are they not written in the
Book of the History of the Kings of Israel?" stays a question at L and is a
statement at B and R. The book's name must not be confused with the title of
Chronicles in the language:
- French: *Le reste des actes de X ... est écrit dans le livre des Annales des
  rois d’Israël* (L *n’est-il pas écrit ...?*; R *a été écrit*).
- Spanish: *Los demás hechos de X ... están escritos en el libro de la Historia
  de los Reyes de Israel* (L *¿no están escritos en el libro de los hechos de
  los días de los reyes de Israel?*; R *Todo lo demás que hizo X está escrito
  en la historia de los reyes de Israel*).
- Swahili: *Mambo mengine ya X ... yameandikwa katika Kitabu cha Historia ya
  Wafalme wa Israeli* (L *je, hayajaandikwa ...?*).
- Vietnamese: *Các việc khác của X ... đều được chép trong Sách Lịch Sử Các Vua
  Y-sơ-ra-ên* (L *chẳng phải đã được chép ... sao?*).
- Korean: B X의 나머지 행적은 ... 이스라엘 왕 실록에 기록되어 있다; R 이스라엘
  왕들의 역사를 적은 책 (uncertain); L ... 기록되어 있지 않으냐?

Chronicles names its sources differently ("the book of the kings of Israel
and Judah", "the words of Nathan the prophet", "the vision of Iddo the
seer"): keep the same pattern.

**Death and burial.** "X rested with his ancestors and was buried with them
in the City of David."
- French L *se coucha avec ses pères*, B *reposa avec ses ancêtres*, R *est
  mort et a été enterré dans la cité de David*.
- Spanish L *durmió con sus padres*, B *descansó con sus antepasados*, R
  *murió*; *y fue sepultado en la ciudad de David*.
- Swahili L *alilala pamoja na baba zake*, B *alipumzika pamoja na babu zake*,
  R *alikufa*; *akazikwa katika mji wa Daudi*.
- Vietnamese L *nằm xuống với tổ phụ mình*, B *an giấc cùng tổ tiên*, R *qua
  đời*; *và được chôn trong thành Đa-vít*.
- Korean L 조상들과 함께 누웠고, B 조상들과 함께 잠들었고, R 죽었고; 다윗 성에
  묻혔다.

**The successor.** French *et Y son fils régna à sa place* (R *son fils Y est
devenu roi à sa place*); Spanish L *y reinó en su lugar Y su hijo*, B *Su hijo
Y reinó en su lugar*, R *Su hijo Y lo sucedió como rey*; Swahili L *na Y
mwanawe akatawala mahali pake*, B, R *na mwanawe Y akawa mfalme mahali pake*;
Vietnamese *Y con trai vua lên kế vị*; Korean L 그를 대신하여 왕이 되었다, B 그의
아들 Y가 뒤를 이어 왕이 되었다.

**The high places.** "The high places were not taken away; the people still
sacrificed and burned incense on the high places": French L *les hauts lieux
ne furent pas supprimés*, B *ne disparurent pas*; *le peuple offrait encore
des sacrifices et brûlait de l’encens sur les hauts lieux*; Spanish *los
lugares altos no fueron quitados; el pueblo todavía ofrecía sacrificios y
quemaba incienso en los lugares altos*; Swahili *mahali pa juu hapakuondolewa;
watu waliendelea kutoa dhabihu na kufukiza uvumba mahali pa juu*; Vietnamese
*các nơi cao không bị dẹp bỏ; dân chúng vẫn dâng sinh tế và đốt hương trên các
nơi cao*; Korean 산당들은 없어지지 않아서 백성은 여전히 산당에서 제사를 드리고
분향했다.

**"To this day"** (the writer's own day): French L *jusqu’à ce jour*, B, R
*jusqu’à aujourd’hui*; Spanish *hasta el día de hoy*; Swahili L *mpaka siku
hii ya leo*, B, R *mpaka leo*; Vietnamese *cho đến ngày nay*; Korean 오늘날까지.

**"The word of the LORD came to X"**: French *la parole du SEIGNEUR fut
adressée à X*; Spanish *la palabra del SEÑOR vino a X*; Swahili *neno la BWANA
likamjia X*; Vietnamese *lời CHÚA đến với X*; Korean L 주의 말씀이 X에게 임하여
말씀하셨다, B 주의 말씀이 X에게 임했다, R 주께서 X에게 말씀하셨다.

## Address forms

As the Samuel note. Added for these books:
- Vietnamese: subjects say *chúa tôi là vua* (L), *tâu bệ hạ* (B), *thưa đức
  vua* (R), and call themselves *đầy tớ* or *tớ gái của vua* (L, B), *tôi* (R);
  prophets call the king *vua*, and the king calls a prophet *ông*, but
  *cha* where the Hebrew says "my father" (2 Kings 6:21; 13:14); Ahab and
  Jezebel are *anh* and *em* at B and R.
- Korean: prophets speak to kings in 합쇼체 unless the scene sets otherwise
  (Elijah to Ahab may be plain); the king says "my father" to Elisha as 내
  아버지여.

## Settled while drafting

AI-draft choices recorded so later work follows them; reviewers may change
any of them. Each is also a rendering decision at its first use in each
block of chapters.

- **All five**: a name keeps the spelling of the verse it is in, so
  Chronicles' own forms stand beside Kings' (Huram and Hiram, Abijah and
  Abijam, Ornan and Araunah, Hadoram and Adoram, Tilgath-pilneser at L).
  The genealogies (1 Chronicles 1–9) keep one list formula per level ("the
  sons of X: A, B and C"; "fathered" at L, "was the father of" at B, R;
  "father of" a town becomes "founder of" at R) and take the published
  Genesis forms of every name Genesis has. The temple refrain "for he is
  good; his steadfast love endures forever" keeps each language's published
  Psalm 136 wording. Josiah's laments (2 Chronicles 35:25) are named so
  they are not taken for the book of Lamentations. 2 Chronicles 36:22–23 is
  the same Hebrew as Ezra 1:1–3, which can reuse it.
- **"Thus says the LORD"**: French *Ainsi parle le SEIGNEUR* (L), *Le
  SEIGNEUR dit ceci* (B), *Voici ce que dit le SEIGNEUR* (R); Spanish *Así
  dice el SEÑOR* / *El SEÑOR dice esto* / *Esto es lo que dice el SEÑOR*;
  Swahili *BWANA asema hivi* (L), *Hivi ndivyo BWANA asemavyo* (B), *Hili
  ndilo BWANA analosema* (R), as in 2 Samuel.
- **French**: *serviteur* for God's named servants at every level; the
  priestly divisions are *classes*, as Luke 1:5 (*classe d'Abia*).
- **Spanish**: the priestly divisions are *turnos*, as Luke 1:5; the high
  places are *lugares altos* (L, B), *santuarios sobre las colinas* (R).
- **Swahili**: *ugonjwa (mbaya) wa ngozi* for the skin disease, never
  *ukoma*; deity spans only on free pronouns and possessives.
- **Vietnamese**: God's servants (Moses, David, the prophets, "your
  servant" in prayer) are *đầy tớ* at every level, Literal included; *nô lệ*
  only for slavery itself. The sources are never *Sử Ký*, the title of
  Chronicles.
- **Korean**: years, ages and large numbers in figures; the source books are
  …왕 실록 at B (never 역대기, the title of Chronicles); prophets speak to kings
  in 합쇼체.

## Before review

Only rendering decisions were added; the meaning decisions are the English
records' own. Reviewers should look first at the decisions marked uncertain,
the regnal formulas, the kings with two names, the genealogies of
1 Chronicles 1–9, and the address forms (which brother is older, in
Vietnamese and Korean, is often a guess). Known unevenness between blocks,
left for a reviewer:

- Numbers above one hundred are in words in some blocks and figures in
  others (French, Swahili and Vietnamese 1 Kings 1–11 against the rest).
- (Resolved) French "Your Majesty" at R is now *ô roi* in 2 Kings 1–12 too,
  and 2 Samuel 8:16 now says *archiviste* for the recorder.
- Swahili *agano* and *mkataba* for a treaty. (The valley of Rephaim is now
  *Bonde la Refaimu* everywhere, 2 Samuel 5 included.)
- Vietnamese judges of Israel now follow the Joshua–Ruth note: *quan xét*
  (L, B), *thủ lĩnh* (R), where the drafts had *thẩm phán*.
- Korean measures say 약 X in some blocks and X쯤 in others.
