# Amharic: 1–2 Kings, 1–2 Chronicles and Ezra

**Status: proposed, awaiting a native Amharic reviewer.** The choices used to
draft 1–2 Kings, 1–2 Chronicles and Ezra in Amharic, so that books drafted in
parallel read as one translation. `docs/amharic-style.md`,
`docs/pentateuch-amharic-terms.md` and `docs/joshua-samuel-amharic-terms.md`
still hold; this list adds what these books need. Decision text stays in
English, quoting the Amharic words.

## Carried over

- Genesis to 2 Samuel are on main in Amharic. Names, places and recurring
  words keep the forms used there: grep before choosing a new one.
- Address to a king: እርስዎ, ጌታዬ ንጉሥ, ባሪያዎ (L) / አገልጋይዎ (B, R); a
  prophet bringing God's word to a king says አንተ; a king's own family say
  አንተ.
- "The LORD of Armies": L `{{divine_name:እግዚአብሔር|ያህዌ}} ጸባኦት`; B, R
  `የሠራዊት አምላክ {{divine_name:እግዚአብሔር|ያህዌ}}`.
- "As the LORD lives": `{{divine_name:እግዚአብሔር|ያህዌ}} ሕያው ነውና` (L, B).
- Kiriath-jearim ቂርያትይዓሪም; compound place names are one word unless main
  already writes them as two (ቤተ ልሔም).

## Parallel passages

- **Chronicles retells Samuel and Kings.** Where the Hebrew is the same,
  Chronicles keeps the Amharic of the Samuel or Kings passage word for word;
  where Chronicles' Hebrew differs (added, left out, changed), it follows
  its own Hebrew and a decision says so. Kings is drafted first for this
  reason.
- 2 Chronicles 36:22–23 and Ezra 1:1–3 are the same decree: one wording.
- Ezra 2 and Nehemiah 7 list the same returnees; Ezra comes first and sets
  the names.
- 1 Chronicles 1–9 retell the genealogies of Genesis, Exodus, Numbers,
  Joshua, Ruth and Samuel: names keep the forms on main.
- New Testament quotations and echoes keep the Amharic New Testament's
  wording where the Hebrew agrees: 1 Kings 19:10, 14, 18 (Romans 11:3–4);
  the queen of the South (Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31); Elijah and the widow,
  Elisha and Naaman (Luke 4:25–27).

**Ezra 4:8–6:18 and 7:12–26 are in Aramaic.** Translate from the Aramaic as
the source gives it; the terms and names are the same as in the Hebrew
chapters.
