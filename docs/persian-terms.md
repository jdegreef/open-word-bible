# Persian terms: Genesis to Revelation

**Status: proposed, awaiting native Persian reviewers.** Nothing in Persian
is drafted yet; every form below is a first proposal, written with the
style guide before drafting so that books drafted in parallel agree. Every
Persian record will be `ai_draft`. If a choice here changes, every passage
drafted under it is revised.

Reviewers: start with
[`docs/review-notes/fa-review-checklist.md`](review-notes/fa-review-checklist.md).

## How this glossary relates to the style guide

`docs/persian-style.md` is the brief for drafting Persian and holds in full:
the three levels (تحت‌اللفظی, متعادل, روان), address, the divine name and
the setting spans, spelling and the half-space, punctuation, numbers, the
words for the whole Bible (section 8) and the formulas (section 11). This
glossary is the single reference for the words, formulas and names the
whole Bible needs beyond those. `docs/hindi-terms.md`,
`docs/indonesian-terms.md` and `docs/chinese-terms.md` are its counterparts
in other languages; each book's own terms note (`docs/isaiah-terms.md` and
the others) still settles the meaning, and this file settles only the
Persian wording.

Where a form is marked *alt.*, that is the main alternative a reviewer
should weigh. Where this translation's New Testament or an earlier book
already has a form, that form wins. Decision text stays in English, quoting
the Persian words.

Below, *dn* is `{{divine_name:خداوند|یهوه}}`. Levels are L (تحت‌اللفظی), B
(متعادل) and R (روان).

## Drafting

- Translate from the Hebrew (and the Aramaic) and the Greek, using the
  literal gloss, the English and the meaning decisions to see how a
  sentence was read. Never from a reference Bible. **The Old Persian
  Version (ترجمهٔ قدیم) is for comparison only**, though it is in the public
  domain; Muzhdeh, the New Millennium Version, the Persian Contemporary
  Bible and the Dari versions likewise.
- The vocabulary in this file is the church's shared vocabulary, and the
  names are the conventional forms; using them is not copying wording.
- Keep every `\v N ` marker where the English has it, and the lines (`\q `)
  as the English level has them.
- Quotation marks « » outside and “ ” inside, typed in logical order. Each
  level opens and closes the same quotations as the English level.
- Settings mirror the English spans where Persian has the same choice:
  `divine_name`, `units`, and `gender` where Persian really has two
  wordings. No `deity_pronoun` and no `christos` spans.
- Persian ی and ک, the half-space (U+200C), هٔ for the ezafe after a silent
  ه, no vowel signs (style guide section 7).

## Address and pronouns

The style guide section 5 holds in full; in brief:

- **People speaking or praying to God**: تو, at every level.
- **God speaking**: من; to people تو / شما, one person or many.
- **To a king**: ای پادشاه (L, B), اعلیحضرت is not used; شما at B and R.
  "My lord the king": سرورم، پادشاه (L, B, R). "Long live the king": زنده باد
  پادشاه.
- **To a ruler or master**: تو at L; شما at B and R. Moses and Aaron say
  شما to Pharaoh at B and R.
- **Family and equals**: تو. Nathan says تو to David ("آن مرد تو هستی!", 2
  Samuel 12:7) at every level.
- **"Your servant" for "I"** (*ʿavdeka*): غلامت / خادمت at L and where the
  English Balanced keeps it (خادم تو at B); من at R.

### Torah, law

تورات for the Torah as a book (the Five Books, "the book of the law of
Moses"); شریعت for "the law" as God's teaching and commands; حکم and
فرمان for single rules. "The law of the LORD" in the Psalms: شریعت *dn*.

### Sheol and the Pit

Sheol is شئول at L, عالم مردگان at B and R (with a decision once per book);
the Pit (*bor*, *shahat*) is چاه (L), گور / گودال مرگ (B, R) as the English
level.

### Nations and peoples

*goyim* is قوم‌ها (L, B), ملت‌ها where the sense is political, and اقوام
بیگانه / غیر اسرائیلیان (R) where the English Readable says "other nations".
*ʿam* (people, of Israel): قوم.

### Kings and Chronicles: the regnal formulas

- **Accession.** "In the Nth year of X king of Judah, Y son of Z became king
  over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned N years." L: در سال Nم X پادشاه
  یهودا، Y پسر Z در سامره بر اسرائیل پادشاه شد و N سال سلطنت کرد. Numbers in
  words.
- **Verdict.** "He did what was evil / right in the eyes of the LORD": او
  آنچه در نظر *dn* بد / درست بود انجام داد (L, B); کارهایی کرد که *dn* از آنها
  ناخشنود / خشنود بود (R).
- **Source.** "Are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the
  kings of Israel?": آیا در کتاب تاریخ پادشاهان اسرائیل نوشته نشده است؟ (L,
  B); در کتاب تاریخ پادشاهان اسرائیل نوشته شده است (R).
- **Death.** "He slept with his fathers": `به {{gender:نیاکان|پدران}} خود پیوست`
  (L, B) where the English has a span; درگذشت (R).
- **Genealogies.** "X fathered Y": X پدر Y بود (B, R); از X، Y زاده شد (L).

- 4:8 «اسیری را به اسیری برد» at Literal: is the phrase still understood?

### Philippians

- Paul says شما to the Philippians, and تو to the one co-worker in 4:3.
  Address: ای {{gender:برادران و خواهران|برادران}}; in 4:1 Balanced and
  Readable add عزیز.
- Doulos: برده (Literal) / خادم (Balanced, Readable), in 1:1 and 2:7 (صورت
  برده / صورت خادم). Overseers and deacons (1:1): ناظران و شماسان.
- Sarx: bodily life (1:22-24) is جسم / بدن; outward credentials (3:3-4) are
  جسم (Literal) / امتیازهای انسانی (Balanced) / آنچه هستیم یا کرده‌ایم
  (Readable). نفس is kept for sarx against the Spirit.
- Politeuomai and politeuma (1:27, 3:20): چون شهروندان زندگی کنید,
  شهروندی.
- 2:6-11 keeps the English poetry breaks. Harpagmos: چیزی برای بهره‌جویی.
  Kenoō: خود را خالی ساخت.
- Katatomē (3:2): قطع‌کنندگان (Literal), set against ختنه‌شدگان in 3:3.
  Skybala (3:8): فضله.
- Katalambanō (3:12-13): به چنگ آوردن at every level, keeping the play on
  words. Teleios: کامل (Literal) / بالغ (Balanced).
- The drink offering (2:17) is هدیهٔ ریختنی. Apechō (4:18): به‌تمامی
  پرداخت شده / رسید.
- Kaisar (4:22): قیصر (Literal) / امپراتور (Balanced, Readable); the
  praetorium (1:13) is گارد امپراتوری.

Names: فیلیپی, فیلیپیان, تیموتائوس, اپافرودیتوس, افودیه, سنتیخی,
کلیمنتس, بنیامین, مقدونیه, تسالونیکی.

For reviewers:

- فضله for skybala (3:8): is it too coarse for public reading, or is that
  Paul's point?
- 2:7 صورت برده at Literal beside صورت خادم at Balanced: do churches
  expect غلام here?
- قانع for autarkēs (4:11): does it carry contentment that does not depend
  on circumstances?

### Colossians

- Paul says شما to the Colossians, and تو in his word to Archippus (4:17).
- 1:15-20 keeps the English poetry breaks. Eikōn is صورت, prōtotokos
  نخست‌زاده at every level, plērōma پری. Thrones, lordships, rulers and
  authorities (1:16): تخت‌ها، سروری‌ها، ریاست‌ها، قدرت‌ها (Literal).
- Reconcile (1:20-22): آشتی دادن (Literal, Balanced) / نزد خود بازگرداندن
  (Readable), as in 2 Corinthians and Ephesians.
- Stoicheia (2:8, 2:20): عناصر جهان (Literal) / نیروهای بنیادین جهان
  (Balanced) / نیروها و قاعده‌هایی که این جهان را می‌گردانند (Readable).
- Sarx: bodily presence (2:1, 2:5) and Christ's body (1:22) are جسم; the
  sinful self (2:11 Balanced, 2:18, 2:23) is نفس; 2:13 uncircumcision of
  the flesh keeps جسم.
- Cheirographon (2:14): سند دست‌نوشته (Literal) / سند بدهی (Balanced).
  Shadow and body (2:17): سایه ... بدن (Literal) / حقیقت (Balanced).
- Old and new self (3:9-10): انسان کهنه / انسان تازه, as in Ephesians 4.
  Barbarian and Scythian (3:11): بربر, سکایی.
- Household (3:18-4:1) follows Ephesians 5:22-6:9: تسلیم, برده, ارباب
  for owners and the Master in heaven, خداوند for Christ.
- Syndoulos (1:7, 4:7): هم‌بنده (Literal) / هم‌خدمت (Balanced, Readable in
  1:7).

Names: کولسی, تیموتائوس, اپافراس, لائودیکیه, هیراپولیس, تیخیکوس,
اونیسیموس, آریستارخوس, مرقس, برنابا, یوستوس, لوقا, دیماس, نیمفا, آرخیپوس.

For reviewers:

- نخست‌زادهٔ همهٔ آفرینش (1:15) at Literal: does it suggest the Son was
  created? Balanced adds بالاتر از همهٔ آفرینش to show rank.
- پری for plērōma (1:19, 2:9): is کمال or تمامی more natural?
- 2:21 «دست مزن، مچش، لمس مکن» at Literal and Balanced: do the old
  imperatives read well, or should Balanced say «دست نزن، نچش، لمس نکن»?
