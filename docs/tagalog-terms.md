# Tagalog terms: Genesis to Revelation

**Status: drafting glossary, awaiting a native Tagalog reviewer.** Every
Tagalog record is `ai_draft`. The forms below are the forms drafters use,
in every book; if a choice here changes, every passage drafted under it is
revised.

## How this glossary relates to the style guide

`docs/tagalog-style.md` is the brief for drafting Tagalog and holds in
full: the levels, the register, address and *po*, the divine name and the
markers inside its span, the pronouns for God, the reader settings, the
key terms (section 7), Catholic and Protestant usage (section 9),
spelling (section 10), the recurring formulas (section 12a) and the
book-by-book workflow (section 12b). This glossary is the single list of
recurring words and names, so that two books drafted in parallel spell the
same person and say the same thing the same way. Each book's own terms
note (`docs/isaiah-terms.md` and the others) still settles the meaning;
this file settles only the Tagalog wording.

- **One form per person, place and term.** A drafter who needs a name or a
  recurring word not listed here forms it by the rules of the style guide,
  section 6, records it in a decision where it first appears, and adds it
  to "Added while drafting" at the end of this file in the same pull
  request.
- A form marked **(L)**, **(B)** or **(R)** applies at that level only;
  otherwise it holds at every level.
- The names are the Spanish-derived forms shared by the Filipino churches,
  written without accents; they are conventions, not the wording of any
  version, and may be used.
- Personal names take *si*, *ni*, *kay*; places, peoples and titles take
  *ang*, *ng*, *sa*.
- Never type Greek or Hebrew: the source words are given in
  transliteration.
