# Amharic glossary (whole Bible)

**Status: proposed, awaiting a native Amharic reviewer.** One list of the
recurring Amharic choices of the Open Word Bible, merged from the seven
per-batch terms lists (`docs/pentateuch-amharic-terms.md`,
`joshua-samuel-`, `kings-chronicles-ezra-`, `nehemiah-proverbs-`,
`ecclesiastes-lamentations-`, `ezekiel-amos-` and
`obadiah-malachi-amharic-terms.md`) and checked against the text of all 66
books. **This file is the reference**: where it and a per-batch list
disagree, this file wins, and where it is silent the per-batch list and
`docs/amharic-style.md` still hold (the style guide governs the New
Testament words, address, punctuation and settings in detail). Decision text
stays in English, quoting the Amharic.

Where two lists disagreed, the row says so and names the rule, which is the
form the text uses most (counts from the consistency scan of November 2026,
over records × levels). A form that a rendering decision justifies on its
record (a wordplay, a parallel passage, a New Testament quotation, a
different Hebrew word) is not an outlier.

Levels: L Literal (ቃል በቃል), B Balanced (ሚዛናዊ), R Readable (ቀላል).
`{{dn}}` stands for `{{divine_name:እግዚአብሔር|ያህዌ}}`.

## 8. Units

- Literal keeps the original unit; Balanced and Readable give metric by
  default in a `units` span with the original as the alternative, where the
  English converts (`{{units:3 ኪሎ ሜትር ያህል|15 ምዕራፍ}}`); no span where the
  English has none.
- Words for the unit, with ያህል: N ሜትር ያህል, N ሳንቲ ሜትር ያህል (not ሳ.ሜ.), N ኪሎ ያህል,
  N ግራም ያህል, N ሊትር ያህል; decimals with a point (1.10 ሜትር).
- Values: cubit ክንድ 45 ሳንቲ ሜትር (a "long cubit" keeps the English value);
  span ስንዝር 23 ሳንቲ ሜትር; handbreadth መዳፍ 7.5 ሳንቲ ሜትር; shekel ሰቅል 11.5 ግራም;
  beka ቤካ 5.7 ግራም; gerah ጌራ 0.6 ግራም; mina ምናን 0.6 ኪሎ; talent መክሊት 34 ኪሎ;
  ephah ኢፍ 22 ሊትር; bath ባዶስ 22 ሊትር; omer ጎሞር 2.2 ሊትር; seah ሲዓ 7 ሊትር; hin
  ኢን 3.7 ሊትር; homer and kor ቆሮስ 220 ሊትር.
- Coins (New Testament): a `units` span gives the value in wages with the coin
  as the alternative (`{{units:የአንድ ቀን ደመወዝ|አንድ ዲናር}}`); silver pieces
  የብር ሳንቲም, so ብር does not read as the birr.
- Clock time: L keeps the count; B, R give the hour as Ethiopians count from
  dawn where it matches (ከቀኑ ስድስት ሰዓት).

## 9. Address and register

- God and Jesus in the narrative: the plain singular (እርሱ, ኢየሱስ መለሰ).
  People pray to God and speak to Jesus with አንተ; "your servant" in prayer
  ባሪያህ (L) / አገልጋይህ (B, R).
- God speaks to one person, or to Israel in the singular, with አንተ (አንቺ to
  a woman or a city addressed as a woman); to a group with እናንተ.
- Subjects, servants, soldiers and vassals to a king: እርስዎ, ጌታዬ ንጉሥ, and
  ባሪያዎ (L) / አገልጋይዎ (B, R) where the Hebrew uses the courtly third person.
  An honoured guest or stranger: ጌታዬ with እርስዎ, the speaker አገልጋይዎ.
- A prophet bringing God's word to a king (Moses to Pharaoh, Samuel to Saul,
  Nathan to David): አንተ. A king to a prophet: እርስዎ where Kings has it, else
  አንተ. Kings to subjects, allied kings, friends and equals: አንተ.
- Family members, parents and in-laws included: አንተ, አንቺ; respect is carried
  by አባቴ, እባክህ. A king's own sons say አንተ to him.
- Ruth to Boaz እርስዎ; he answers አንቺ, ልጄ. The lovers of the Song: አንተ / አንቺ.
- Pronouns and spelling: እርሱ, እርሷ, እነርሱ (not እሱ, እሷ, እነሱ); first person past
  -ኩ after a consonant stem, -ሁ after a vowel stem; ውኃ; ቆ, ቁ rather than ቈ, ቍ;
  the church spellings ኃጢአት, ሥጋ, ጸጋ, ጸሎት, ዓለም, ሐዋርያ, ሰማይ, ቅዱስ.
- Narrative is in the past; Literal keeps the Hebrew chain of "and"; laws at
  L, B in the imperfect or jussive, at R with -አለበት or the imperative.

## 10. Settings

- `divine_name` on every YHWH (and Yah), at every level, as section 1.
- `christos` on every *christos*, as section 1.
- `deity_pronoun`: none in Amharic (no capitals); the first record of a
  passage file whose English has a span says so once.
- `gender`: only where Amharic has two real wordings:
  `{{gender:ወንድሞችና እኅቶች|ወንድሞች}}`, `{{gender:ወንድምህ ወይም እኅትህ|ወንድምህ}}`,
  `{{gender:ሰዎች|ወንዶች}}`, `{{gender:ቅድመ አያቶቻችን|አባቶቻችን}}`. ሰው, ሰዎች, ማንም,
  ልጆች and the generic suffix already include women: no span, and a
  setting_alternative decision once per passage.
- `units`: as section 8.
- Settings change wording, never meaning: a disagreement about meaning is a
  decision or a footnote, not a span.
